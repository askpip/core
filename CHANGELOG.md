# AskPIP Core Changelog

This changelog records significant changes to AskPIP Core in a concise, human-readable form.

Git provides the complete technical history of the repository. This changelog records the major documents, decisions, releases and structural changes that are important to the Founders and future contributors.

- **Pip Knowledge Rules approved, and the Knowledge Curation System's documents altered to follow them (5 October 2026, at a Founder's direction).** A new Standard, `Knowledge Curation System/Standards/Pip_Knowledge_Rules.md` (v0.1), states in one place what Pip may say: what several reputable, independent sources agree on, with the strength shown; the better-supported view first where sources disagree; a range where figures differ; local information only for its place, from one reliable source if need be; a plain statement where no source was found; the plant deciding, not the calendar; no products or amounts, and the precautions always; and nothing of Pip's own. The research does not decide what is true and neither do the Founders: the rules weigh what reputable sources say, and the Founders are asked only about scope, how Pip works, and approval. Amended to follow the rules: Founder Review Dossier Standard v1.4, ROC Operations Manual v2.10, ROC Charter v0.05, KIT Charter v0.03, KIT Operations Manual v0.7, KIT PKR Build Skill v0.2, Knowledge Integration Workflow v0.05, Garden Shed Operations Skill v0.7, PIP AI Loading Guide v0.12, and the briefs-and-forms working note v0.6. The amendments add the Build Check (the ROC reopens the sources and checks every statement Pip will make, after KIT builds and before the Founders approve), and carry in three changes directed on 28 September 2026 that had not reached the repository. The earlier proposal is kept as the record, with an Integration Record. Not yet done: the Decision Briefs and Review Forms in `Working/Founder Review/` still follow earlier patterns and are to be redone one at a time; no Build Check has been run; the new document names are not yet in the naming Standard. Three standing decisions were made by a Founder the same day and are recorded in the rules: Pip uses the device's location where the gardener allows it, and otherwise the gardener says where the plant is in its journal entry; a garden company is an acceptable source for local months, with Pip saying that only one source was found and that months are only a guide; and planting and moving advice belongs in Pip where the research provides it.
