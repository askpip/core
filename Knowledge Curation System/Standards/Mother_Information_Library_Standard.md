# Purpose

The Mother Information Library (MIL) is the permanent repository of Founder-approved horticultural information assets within the Plant Intelligence Platform (PIP).

Its purpose is to preserve approved information in its original approved form while maintaining complete traceability, version history and long-term integrity.

The MIL exists independently of any particular artificial intelligence model, software implementation or operational knowledge structure.

It is the enduring information foundation upon which the Plant Intelligence Platform is built.

---

# Definition

The Mother Information Library is the authoritative repository of Founder-approved horticultural information assets.

Information stored within the MIL represents approved information rather than operational intelligence.

The MIL preserves information exactly as approved by the Founders.

It does not contain draft material, operational intelligence or artificial intelligence interpretations.

---

# Objectives

The Mother Information Library exists to:

- preserve Founder-approved information assets;
- maintain complete traceability of approved information;
- provide a stable source for operational knowledge integration;
- preserve historical versions of approved information;
- support long-term stewardship of horticultural knowledge;
- remain independent of changing technologies and software implementations.

---

# Information Assets

The primary governed record type held within the MIL is the Approved Research Compilation (ARC) — see the PIP System Identity and Naming Standard (SINS-001) §7.3. An ARC is the authoritative, structured representation of Founder-approved information on a defined subject, and is what KIT retrieves from when building a PKR.

Each ARC records the Evidence Confidence Level(s) assigned under the Evidence Assessment Standard (EAS) to its constituent Assessed Finding(s), so that the strength of the underlying evidence remains attached to the information as it moves toward operational use. An ARC does not carry one blended Evidence Confidence Level for all its content; where an ARC holds more than one Assessed Finding, each finding's level is recorded and preserved individually.

Alongside ARCs, the Mother Information Library may contain any other Founder-approved information asset, including but not limited to:

- Founder Review Dossiers (the archived pre-approval submission an ARC was built from);
- source references;
- photographs;
- diagrams;
- illustrations;
- botanical definitions;
- terminology;
- Founder observations;
- research notes approved by the Founders;
- structural relationships;
- biological descriptions;
- supporting documentation;
- historical versions of approved information.

Additional information asset types may be introduced as the Knowledge Curation System evolves.

---

# Excluded Material

The Mother Information Library shall not contain:

- unapproved research;
- draft Founder Review Dossiers;
- draft PIP Knowledge Records;
- published operational intelligence assets;
- artificial intelligence assumptions;
- unsupported conclusions;
- superseded information presented as current information.

Historical versions may be retained where required for traceability.

---

# Governance

The Founders retain sole authority for approving information entering the Mother Information Library.

No artificial intelligence system may approve information for inclusion within the MIL.

Following Founder approval, authorised custodians maintain the Mother Information Library while preserving the approved meaning, integrity and traceability of every information asset.

---

# Custodianship

The PIP Research Origin Curator (ROC) is the custodian of the Mother Information Library.

ROC is responsible for:

- archiving Founder-approved information assets;
- maintaining identifiers and metadata;
- preserving version history;
- maintaining relationships between information assets;
- preserving supporting documentation;
- maintaining organisational integrity.

Custodianship does not include authority to modify Founder-approved meaning or approve new information.

---

# Relationship to the Live Intelligence Library

The Mother Information Library and the Live Intelligence Library serve different purposes.

The Mother Information Library preserves Founder-approved information assets.

The Live Intelligence Library contains Founder-approved operational intelligence assets created from that information.

Information flows from the MIL to the Live Intelligence Library through the Knowledge Integration process.

The Live Intelligence Library does not replace the Mother Information Library, nor does the Mother Information Library function as an operational knowledge repository.

Both libraries are permanent components of the Knowledge Curation System.

---

# Information Integrity

Every information asset within the Mother Information Library shall:

- possess a unique identifier;
- preserve its approved content;
- maintain version history;
- remain traceable to its originating Founder Review Dossier or approval process;
- preserve relationships with associated information assets where applicable.

Information shall never be altered in a manner that obscures its approval history.

---

# Accessibility

The Mother Information Library serves different participants within the Knowledge Curation System in different ways.

The Founders govern the contents of the MIL.

The PIP Research Origin Curator maintains the library.

The PIP Knowledge Integration Technician retrieves approved information for operational integration.

The Plant Intelligence Platform does not retrieve operational guidance directly from the Mother Information Library.

Operational guidance is retrieved exclusively from the Live Intelligence Library.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Mother Information Library operates according to the following principles:

- Preservation before convenience.
- Information before interpretation.
- Traceability before efficiency.
- Integrity before automation.
- Stewardship before ownership.
- Long-term maintainability.
- Technology independence.

---

# Success Criteria

The Mother Information Library is successful when:

- every approved information asset can be located, identified and retrieved;
- all information remains fully traceable to Founder approval;
- historical versions are preserved without ambiguity;
- approved information remains independent of operational implementation;
- the Knowledge Integration process can reliably produce operational intelligence from approved information.

---

# Founding Statement

The Mother Information Library exists to preserve the enduring body of Founder-approved horticultural information upon which the Plant Intelligence Platform is built.

By protecting information from alteration, preserving complete traceability and maintaining a permanent repository of approved information assets, the MIL provides the stable foundation from which trustworthy operational intelligence can be developed, maintained and delivered to gardeners through Ask Pip for generations to come.
