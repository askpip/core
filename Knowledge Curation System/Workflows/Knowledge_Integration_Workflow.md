# Purpose

The PIP Knowledge Integration Workflow defines how horticultural information moves through the Knowledge Curation System from initial research commission to operational use within Ask Pip.

Its purpose is to ensure that:

- research is conducted within an authorised scope;
- information is reviewed before entering the Mother Information Library;
- operational intelligence is published only from Founder-approved information, and a draft built before approval stays a draft;
- every PIP Knowledge Record receives Founder approval before publication;
- published operational intelligence remains traceable to its supporting evidence;
- uncertainty, revisions and corrections are handled through controlled processes;
- no artificial intelligence role assumes Founder authority.

This workflow governs both the creation of new operational intelligence and the revision of existing operational intelligence.

---

# Workflow Philosophy

The Knowledge Integration Workflow is founded upon the principle that information should not move directly from research into operational use.

Research, integration, checking, approval, preservation and publication are separate stages.

Each stage has a distinct purpose, responsible participant and authority boundary.

The workflow therefore requires two Founder approvals:

1. **Information Approval** — approval of information for preservation within the Mother Information Library.
2. **Operational Approval** — approval of structured operational intelligence for publication within the Live Intelligence Library.

No operational intelligence asset may bypass either approval.

The Founders give both in one decision. They read Pip's Answers, which shows every word Pip would say from the research, and approve once (Pip Knowledge Rules §7, "One approval"). So that this one decision is an informed one, the research is completed, the draft PIP Knowledge Records are built from it, and every statement is checked against its sources, all before the Founders are asked. Nothing enters the Mother Information Library as approved, and nothing is published, until they have decided.

# Primary Participants

## The Founders

The Founders:

- commission or authorise research;
- define research scope;
- read Pip's Answers, with the Founder Review Dossier and the draft PIP Knowledge Records open to them;
- approve information for inclusion within the Mother Information Library;
- give both approvals in one decision on Pip's Answers;
- approve operational intelligence for publication;
- resolve governance questions and material uncertainties;
- authorise retirement or replacement of published operational intelligence where required.

The Founders retain final authority throughout the workflow.

---

## PIP Research Origin Curator

The PIP Research Origin Curator (ROC):

- conducts authorised research;
- evaluates and organises source material;
- prepares Founder Review Dossiers;
- identifies uncertainty and conflicting evidence;
- responds to Founder requests for further research;
- archives Founder-approved information within the Mother Information Library;
- maintains associated metadata, identifiers and version history;
- passes each completed Founder Review Dossier to KIT for the build;
- carries out the Build Check before the Founders are asked;
- notifies KIT when approved information is available for integration.

ROC does not approve information or create operational intelligence.

---

## PIP Knowledge Integration Technician

The PIP Knowledge Integration Technician (KIT):

- receives each completed Founder Review Dossier from ROC for the build, and retrieves Founder-approved information from the Mother Information Library;
- creates draft PIP Knowledge Records;
- establishes relationships between PKRs;
- preserves source and evidence traceability;
- submits draft PKRs for Founder review;
- implements Founder-approved corrections;
- publishes approved PKRs to the Live Intelligence Library;
- maintains PKR identifiers, metadata, relationships and version history;
- identifies existing PKRs affected by newly approved information.

KIT does not conduct original research or approve PKRs.

---

## Mother Information Library

The Mother Information Library preserves Founder-approved information assets.

It is the authoritative repository that published operational intelligence traces to.

The MIL does not contain draft or published PKRs.

---

## Live Intelligence Library

The Live Intelligence Library preserves Founder-approved operational intelligence assets.

It contains published PKRs available for retrieval and use by the Plant Intelligence Platform.

The LIL does not contain unapproved or draft PKRs.

# Primary Knowledge Lifecycle

The complete knowledge lifecycle, in the order it runs, is:

| Order | What happens | Who | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A research need or knowledge gap is identified | The Founders, or any participant | 1 |
| 2 | Research is commissioned | The Founders | 2 |
| 3 | Research, evidence assessment and the Founder Review Dossier | ROC | 3, 4 |
| 4 | Integration assessment and draft PIP Knowledge Records, built from the completed dossier | KIT | 7, 8, 9 |
| 5 | Build Check: every source reopened and every statement checked; the dossier and the drafts corrected | ROC, with KIT correcting the drafts | 9A |
| 6 | One Founder decision on Pip's Answers, giving information approval and operational approval together | The Founders | 5, 10 |
| 7 | The approved research is archived in the Mother Information Library as an ARC | ROC | 6 |
| 8 | The approved PKRs are published to the Live Intelligence Library | KIT | 11 |
| 9 | The Plant Intelligence Platform retrieves them, and Pip communicates them to the gardener | PIP, Pip | 12 |

The Founder decision at order 6 may be:

- **Approve** — the lifecycle continues to order 7.
- **Approve with changes** — the changes are made and any statement whose meaning changed is checked again. Where ROC or KIT has to write new wording, or a finding or its level changes, the changed statements are shown to the Founder, and accepted, first. The lifecycle then continues to order 7.
- **Not yet** — nothing is archived or published. The work returns to ROC for more research, or to KIT for different wording, or is closed, as the Founders direct.

The stages below keep their numbers. They are described in number order, which is no longer the order in which they run; the table above governs the order.

---

# Stage 1 — Knowledge Need Identification

The workflow begins when a legitimate knowledge need is identified.

A knowledge need may arise from:

- a planned plant profile;
- a new PIP capability;
- a recognised gap in existing knowledge;
- a gardener question that cannot be answered reliably;
- a new horticultural issue;
- a regional or seasonal variation;
- Founder observation;
- new evidence affecting existing information;
- an error or inconsistency identified within the LIL.

The knowledge need shall be described clearly enough to support a defined research commission.

Where the need is too broad, it should be divided into manageable research commissions.

---

# Stage 2 — Research Commission

The Founders authorise a Research Commission that defines:

- the subject;
- the research question;
- the intended purpose;
- the scope;
- exclusions;
- required outputs;
- relevant plant type, region or condition;
- known uncertainties;
- any specific evidence requirements.

ROC shall conduct research only within the authorised scope.

Where ROC identifies a significant issue outside that scope, it may report the issue to the Founders but shall not independently expand the commission.

---

# Stage 3 — Research and Evidence Collection

ROC identifies and evaluates relevant trustworthy sources.

Research may include:

- books;
- scientific literature;
- university extension publications;
- recognised horticultural organisations;
- expert publications;
- approved visual evidence;
- Founder observations;
- other authorised sources.

ROC shall preserve:

- source identity;
- authorship;
- publication details;
- relevant page, section or location;
- access date where applicable;
- evidence context;
- conflicting viewpoints;
- limitations;
- uncertainty.

ROC shall distinguish clearly between:

- source evidence;
- common horticultural practice;
- expert interpretation;
- ROC synthesis;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- Founder observations.

---

# Stage 4 — Founder Review Dossier Preparation

ROC organises the research into a Founder Review Dossier.

The dossier should:

- answer the authorised research question;
- remain within scope;
- present evidence clearly;
- preserve source traceability;
- identify conflicting or weak evidence;
- separate observation from interpretation;
- avoid unsupported conclusions;
- identify information suitable for possible operational use;
- identify matters requiring Founder judgement.

When the dossier is complete, ROC passes it to KIT for integration assessment and the build (Stages 7 and 8). It is not submitted to the Founders on its own. It reaches them at Stage 10, behind Pip's Answers.

The dossier remains unapproved until the Founders decide.

Draft dossiers shall not enter the Mother Information Library as approved information assets.

---

# Stage 5 — Founder Information Review

The Founders' information approval is given at Stage 10, in the same decision as operational approval. This stage describes what that decision means for the information.

The purpose of information approval is to determine whether the information is sufficiently accurate, relevant, balanced and traceable to enter the Mother Information Library. The Founders are not required to read the Founder Review Dossier to give it. They read Pip's Answers, which shows what Pip would say from the dossier's findings after every statement has been checked against its sources. The dossier is open to them throughout.

The Founders are not asked for a decision on each finding, or for a choice between answers where the rules give one: a finding's Evidence Confidence Level sets whether Pip may use it (Pip Knowledge Rules §3). A question of scope, or of how Pip works, that the rules cannot settle is put to a Founder before the build.

For the information, the Founders' decision may mean one of the following. On the Pip's Answers Form, "Approve" gives the first; "Approve with changes" gives the second or the fourth, as the Founder writes; "Not yet" gives the third or the fifth, as the Founder's reason says.

## Approved

The dossier, or specified information within it, is approved for inclusion within the Mother Information Library.

Approval should identify any exclusions, limitations or preserved uncertainties.

## Approved with Limitations

Information may be approved while retaining uncertainty, regional limitations, conditional wording or restrictions on operational use.

Such limitations shall remain attached to the approved information.

## Additional Research Required

The Founders return the dossier to ROC with specific questions or evidence requirements.

ROC revises the dossier or prepares a supplementary dossier.

KIT revises the draft PKRs that rest on what changed, ROC checks them again, and the revised work returns to Founder review through a revised Pip's Answers.

## Partially Approved

The Founders may approve some information while withholding or rejecting other portions.

The approved and unapproved portions shall be distinguishable.

Only the approved information enters the MIL as current approved material.

## Rejected

The information is not authorised for inclusion as approved information.

Rejected information may be retained separately for audit or historical context where directed by the Founders, but it shall not be represented as approved.

---

# Stage 6 — Archiving Within the Mother Information Library

Following explicit Founder approval (Stage 10), ROC creates or updates the Approved Research Compilation (ARC) representing the approved information, with every correction the Build Check made, and archives it within the Mother Information Library together with its supporting records.

Archiving shall include:

- the Approved Research Compilation (ARC) itself — the primary governed record KIT will retrieve from;
- the approved Founder Review Dossier it was built from;
- approved supporting information assets;
- source references;
- approved images, diagrams or illustrations;
- approval status;
- approval date;
- applicable limitations;
- unique identifiers;
- version information;
- relationships to associated assets;
- historical or superseded material where applicable.

ROC shall preserve the information exactly as approved.

ROC may organise, classify and describe the approved assets but shall not alter their approved meaning.

When archiving is complete, ROC notifies KIT, and KIT publishes the approved PKRs (Stage 11).

---

# Stage 7 — Integration Assessment

KIT reviews the completed Founder Review Dossier that ROC has passed to it, or newly approved or revised MIL information, to determine:

- which operational intelligence assets are required;
- whether new PKRs are needed;
- whether existing PKRs require revision;
- which PKR types are appropriate;
- what relationships should be created;
- which sources support each factual PKR;
- whether image, terminology, definition or source PKRs are required;
- whether the information contains unresolved integration questions.

KIT shall not fill information gaps through inference.

Where information is insufficient, contradictory or structurally unclear, KIT shall refer the matter to the Founders.

The Founders may:

- clarify the intended meaning;
- direct KIT to preserve the uncertainty;
- commission further ROC research;
- restrict the proposed operational use.

---

# Stage 8 — Draft PKR Creation

KIT transforms the findings of the completed dossier, or Founder-approved MIL information, into draft PIP Knowledge Records.

A draft PKR built from a dossier the Founders have not yet approved is built so that the Founders can see Pip's words when they decide. It remains a draft, and is not published, until the Founders approve and the research is archived (Stages 10, 6 and 11).

Each draft PKR shall:

- represent a defined operational intelligence asset;
- possess a unique draft identifier;
- use the required PKR structure;
- preserve the meaning of the dossier's findings or the approved information;
- remain traceable to the dossier's findings and, once published, to relevant MIL assets;
- identify supporting Source PKRs or source assets;
- maintain appropriate relationships with other PKRs;
- preserve limitations and uncertainty;
- avoid unsupported operational conclusions;
- express its content as declarative data rather than executable logic; and
- be accompanied by a Founder Review Rendering presenting its complete content in plain language, per the PKR Standard §9, so it can be reviewed without reading its underlying stored format.

Draft PKRs may include:

- definitions;
- terminology;
- plant structures;
- biological processes;
- observations;
- rules;
- exceptions;
- conditions;
- questions;
- images;
- sources;
- relationships;
- other approved PKR types.

Draft PKRs shall remain outside the published LIL until Founder approval is complete.

---

# Stage 9 — Evidence Traceability

Every factual PKR shall be traceable to the findings it was built from and, once published, to supporting approved information.

Where the Plant Intelligence Platform may need to explain, cite or present supporting evidence, the PKR shall link to one or more relevant Source PKRs or other approved operational intelligence assets.

A Source PKR may represent:

- a book;
- a chapter or relevant section;
- a scientific paper;
- an extension publication;
- a recognised horticultural publication;
- a Founder observation;
- an approved image source;
- another approved evidential origin.

The Source PKR should contain sufficient information to:

- identify the source;
- explain its relevance;
- locate the supporting material;
- trace the source back to the MIL;
- support explanation to the gardener where appropriate.

The existence of a Source PKR does not transfer the full source document into the LIL.

It provides an operationally retrievable representation of the approved source information.

---

# Stage 9A — Build Check

Before the Founders are asked anything, ROC checks the work in sessions that took no part in it (ROC Operations Manual Chapter 14). It reopens every source the findings rely on and looks for each recorded quotation on its page. It then checks every statement in the draft PKRs against the finding it cites and against what the pages were confirmed to say.

A statement that fails is corrected by KIT and checked again, until none fails. Where the check shows the dossier says more than its sources, ROC corrects the dossier first, and the drafts follow the dossier as corrected.

---

# Stage 10 — Founder Review: One Decision

ROC and KIT submit **Pip's Answers** and its one-question Form to the Founders.

Pip's Answers shows every word gardeners would see, which is the Founder Review Rendering of every draft PKR in the package (PKR Standard §9), then a short list of what a Founder should know, then the result of the Build Check. The dossier, the PKR package and the check results are available to the Founders, who are not required to read them, or to read or verify a draft PKR's underlying stored format.

The Founders' one decision gives both approvals this workflow requires: information approval (Stage 5) and operational approval. The purpose of the review is to determine whether what Pip would say:

- accurately represents the research;
- preserves intended meaning;
- uses suitable terminology;
- contains appropriate conditions and limitations;
- maintains sufficient evidence traceability;
- establishes correct relationships;
- is suitable for retrieval and use by PIP;
- can be communicated safely and clearly to gardeners.

The decision is one of three.

## Approve

The research is approved for the Mother Information Library (Stage 6) and the PKRs are authorised for publication within the Live Intelligence Library (Stage 11). Approval is granted against the wording in Pip's Answers; the published PKRs' content shall be identical in meaning to it.

## Approve with Changes

The Founders approve subject to changes they state: to wording, to what is included, or to a finding. KIT and ROC make the changes. A statement whose meaning changed is checked again (Stage 9A). The work then proceeds as approved, without a second submission, where the Founder gave the new wording or asked for a statement to be removed. Where ROC or KIT has to write new wording, or a finding or its level changes, the changed statements are shown to the Founder, and accepted, before anything is filed or published.

## Not Yet

Nothing is archived or published. The Founders' reason says what is to happen:

- **Revision required.** KIT revises structure, wording, relationships, traceability or representation, the changed statements are checked again, and Pip's Answers is submitted again.
- **Further research required.** The Founders commission ROC to conduct additional research. The new information goes through the dossier, the build and the Build Check before it returns to the Founders.
- **Rejected.** The information is not approved and the draft PKRs are not published. They may be revised, replaced or closed as directed by the Founders.
- **Approval deferred.** The PKRs may remain in draft where related information, images, relationships or decisions are not yet complete. No deferred PKR may be published as approved operational intelligence.

---

# Stage 11 — Publication to the Live Intelligence Library

Following explicit Founder approval (Stage 10), and once ROC has archived the approved research in the Mother Information Library (Stage 6), KIT publishes the approved PKR to the Live Intelligence Library.

Publication shall include:

- the approved PKR content;
- unique permanent identifier;
- PKR type;
- approval status;
- approval date;
- version number;
- supporting source relationships;
- relevant MIL traceability;
- relationships to other PKRs;
- applicable plant, region, season, age or condition classifications;
- any preserved uncertainty or restrictions;
- publication date.

KIT shall verify that no unapproved draft content is included during publication.

Once published, the PKR becomes an approved operational intelligence asset available for platform retrieval.

---

# Stage 12 — Platform Retrieval and Gardener Communication

The Plant Intelligence Platform retrieves approved operational intelligence from the Live Intelligence Library.

PIP may:

- retrieve relevant PKRs;
- follow relationships between PKRs;
- retrieve definitions and terminology;
- retrieve supporting sources;
- retrieve approved images;
- identify exceptions and conditions;
- request missing observations from the gardener;
- combine compatible PKRs within approved operational boundaries.

PIP shall not:

- create new horticultural truth;
- override PKR limitations;
- conceal uncertainty;
- treat absent information as confirmed;
- retrieve unapproved drafts;
- use the MIL as an unrestricted runtime knowledge source.

Pip communicates the retrieved operational intelligence to the gardener in clear, educational language.

---

# Workflow for Updating Existing Information

Approved information may require revision when:

- new evidence becomes available;
- an existing source is corrected or superseded;
- regional differences become known;
- terminology changes;
- Founder observations reveal an omission;
- user experience reveals ambiguity;
- a published PKR is found to be incomplete;
- new platform capabilities require additional structure.

The update workflow is:

New Evidence or Revision Need

        │

        ▼

Founder Assessment

        │

        ▼

ROC Research Commission

        │

        ▼

Revised or Supplementary Founder Review Dossier

        │

        ▼

KIT Impact Assessment

        │

        ▼

Draft New or Revised PKRs

        │

        ▼

Build Check

        │

        ▼

One Founder Decision on Pip's Answers

        │

        ▼

New MIL Version

        │

        ▼

Publish New PKR Versions

        │

        ▼

Retire or Supersede Previous Versions

The Founders' one decision on Pip's Answers gives both approvals, as at Stage 10: the draft new or revised PKRs are built from the revised dossier and checked before the Founders are asked, and the new MIL version is archived when they approve.

A new MIL version shall not automatically alter any published PKR.

KIT must assess the operational impact, create controlled revisions and obtain Founder approval before the LIL is changed.

---

# Impact Assessment

When new or revised information enters the MIL, KIT shall identify all potentially affected PKRs.

Impact assessment may include:

- PKRs directly derived from the revised information;
- PKRs linked to an affected Source PKR;
- definitions or terminology referenced by other PKRs;
- rules dependent upon an updated condition;
- images or observations affected by reclassification;
- explanations presented to gardeners;
- related plant profiles.

KIT shall document whether each affected PKR:

- remains valid;
- requires revision;
- requires additional evidence;
- should be replaced;
- should be retired;
- requires no action.

Material impact decisions remain subject to Founder review.

---

# Error Correction Workflow

An error may be identified by:

- a Founder;
- ROC;
- KIT;
- a gardener;
- an expert reviewer;
- a platform test;
- an audit;
- new evidence.

The error shall first be classified.

## Presentation Error

Examples include:

- spelling;
- formatting;
- broken links;
- incorrect display metadata;
- non-substantive indexing issues.

Where the approved meaning is unchanged, KIT may correct the technical presentation according to authorised procedures while preserving an audit record.

## Operational Representation Error

Examples include:

- a PKR does not accurately represent approved MIL information;
- a condition was omitted;
- a relationship is incorrect;
- source traceability is incomplete.

KIT prepares a corrected draft PKR for Founder operational approval.

## Information Error

Examples include:

- approved MIL information appears factually incorrect;
- a source was misrepresented;
- new evidence materially contradicts approved information.

The issue returns to Founder assessment and, where required, a ROC research commission.

Affected PKRs may be temporarily restricted or suspended where continued use may mislead gardeners.

---

# Temporary Restriction and Suspension

The Founders may direct that a published PKR be temporarily restricted or suspended where:

- its reliability is under review;
- material supporting evidence is disputed;
- an error may affect gardener safety or plant health;
- an important condition is missing;
- a linked source has become unreliable;
- the PKR is no longer appropriate for operational use.

A suspended PKR shall:

- remain preserved for traceability;
- be unavailable for ordinary operational retrieval;
- display its suspension status;
- record the reason for suspension;
- identify the authorising Founder decision;
- remain subject to review, revision, replacement or retirement.

KIT shall not independently suspend a PKR except through an emergency mechanism explicitly authorised by the Founders.

---

# PKR Revision

A published PKR shall not be silently overwritten.

When its approved operational meaning changes:

- a new version shall be created;
- the previous version shall be preserved;
- the relationship between versions shall be recorded;
- the reason for revision shall be documented;
- affected relationships shall be reviewed;
- Founder approval shall be obtained;
- the new version shall become the active version only after publication.

Minor non-substantive technical corrections may follow a simplified controlled procedure if authorised by the relevant standards.

---

# PKR Retirement

A PKR may be retired when:

- it is no longer accurate;
- it has been replaced by another PKR;
- its terminology is obsolete;
- its purpose has been absorbed into a revised structure;
- it is no longer suitable for operational use;
- the Founders determine that it should be withdrawn.

Retirement shall not erase the PKR.

A retired PKR shall retain:

- its identifier;
- approval and publication history;
- version history;
- retirement date;
- reason for retirement;
- replacement relationship where applicable;
- supporting traceability.

Retired PKRs shall not be used for normal gardener guidance.

---

# Preservation of Uncertainty

Uncertainty may exist at any stage of the workflow.

Uncertainty shall be:

- stated clearly;
- preserved through information approval;
- represented accurately in relevant PKRs;
- communicated by Pip where material;
- linked to any conditions or evidence limitations;
- reviewed when new evidence becomes available.

The workflow shall not force uncertain information into false certainty merely to complete a plant profile or provide an immediate answer.

Where operational use is not justified, the appropriate response is to request further evidence or inform the gardener that a reliable conclusion cannot yet be reached.

---

# Relationship Management

PKRs may maintain relationships such as:

- supported by;
- defined by;
- applies to;
- exception to;
- part of;
- develops from;
- visually indicated by;
- distinguished from;
- affected by;
- illustrated by;
- supersedes;
- related to.

When a PKR is created, revised, suspended or retired, KIT shall review its material relationships.

A PKR relationship shall not be treated as approved merely because both PKRs have individually been approved.

Material operational relationships should themselves be represented and approved as part of the relevant PKRs or as separate Relationship PKRs.

---

# Source Change Management

Where a source is corrected, withdrawn, superseded or judged unreliable:

1. the relevant MIL source information is reviewed;
2. the Founders determine whether further research is required;
3. ROC prepares revised information where necessary;
4. KIT identifies every linked factual PKR;
5. each affected PKR is assessed;
6. revisions or suspensions are prepared;
7. Founder approval is obtained;
8. approved changes are published.

A source change does not automatically invalidate every linked PKR.

The effect shall be assessed according to the importance of that source to the approved operational intelligence.

---

# New Plant Integration

When a new plant type is introduced, the same workflow applies.

The platform shall not assume that rules from one plant type apply to another.

A new plant integration may require multiple research commissions covering areas such as:

- plant identity;
- structure;
- growth;
- flowering or fruiting;
- age and development;
- seasonal behaviour;
- climate or regional variation;
- health conditions;
- visual observability;
- terminology;
- pruning or care principles;
- exceptions and uncertainty.

Each commission may produce approved MIL information and related PKRs.

A plant profile becomes operational only when sufficient approved PKRs exist to support its intended platform function.

---

# Incomplete Plant Profiles

The existence of some approved PKRs does not imply that a complete operational plant profile exists.

A plant profile may be classified as:

- research in progress;
- information approved;
- integration in progress;
- limited operational release;
- complete for defined scope;
- under revision;
- suspended.

The Founders determine whether the available operational intelligence is sufficient for release.

PIP shall operate only within the approved scope of the plant profile.

---

# Audit Trail

Every significant workflow action shall be traceable.

The audit trail should preserve:

- research commission identity;
- dossier versions;
- Founder review decisions;
- approved and excluded information;
- MIL identifiers and versions;
- draft PKR versions;
- Founder operational decisions;
- publication records;
- revision history;
- suspensions;
- retirements;
- responsible role;
- relevant dates.

The audit trail exists to preserve accountability and support future review.

---

# Authority Boundaries

No participant may bypass the responsibilities of another.

ROC may research and archive approved information but may not approve it or create PKRs.

KIT may structure and publish approved PKRs but may not research horticultural truth or approve operational intelligence.

The Plant Intelligence Platform may retrieve and apply approved PKRs but may not create new horticultural knowledge.

Pip may communicate approved operational intelligence but may not conceal uncertainty or improvise unsupported advice.

The Founders retain authority over information approval, operational approval and material governance decisions.

---

# Workflow Controls

The following controls are mandatory:

- no unapproved information enters the MIL as current approved information;
- no PKR is published from unapproved information: a draft PKR may be built from a completed Founder Review Dossier so that the Founders can see Pip's words when they decide, and it stays a draft until they approve and the research is archived in the MIL;
- no draft PKR is put before the Founders until the Build Check is complete and no statement fails;
- no draft PKR enters the published LIL;
- no published PKR lacks traceability to approved MIL information;
- no material PKR revision bypasses Founder operational approval;
- no superseded version is erased where traceability requires preservation;
- no artificial intelligence role approves its own work;
- no uncertainty is removed without supporting evidence and Founder approval;
- no PKR is submitted for Founder operational review without an accompanying Founder Review Rendering;
- no PKR is published whose content diverges from the Founder Review Rendering approved for it; and
- no PKR carries executable logic or program code embodying horticultural judgement in place of declarative data.

---

# Success Criteria

The Knowledge Integration Workflow is successful when:

- every published operational intelligence asset has passed both Founder approval gates;
- every factual PKR is traceable to approved supporting information;
- the responsibilities of ROC, KIT, the Founders, PIP and Pip remain distinct;
- new evidence can be incorporated without losing historical traceability;
- errors can be corrected through controlled processes;
- operational intelligence remains explainable, maintainable and trustworthy;
- gardeners receive guidance derived only from approved operational intelligence;
- the system remains functional and understandable despite future technological change.

---

# Founding Statement

The PIP Knowledge Integration Workflow exists to ensure that horticultural information progresses from research to gardener guidance through a controlled, transparent and accountable process.

By requiring separate research, integration, independent checking, Founder approval of both the information and its operational form, preservation and publication stages, the workflow protects the integrity of the Plant Intelligence Platform from unsupported assumptions and uncontrolled artificial intelligence interpretation.

Every operational intelligence asset delivered through Ask Pip shall therefore remain connected to its approved information, supporting evidence, decision history and responsible stewardship.

This workflow provides the enduring path through which trustworthy information becomes trustworthy operational intelligence in service of gardeners and the responsible care of creation.
