## Where the sources differ

Seven points of disagreement are on record (Brief section 6). Each is carried in the findings above as it stands, without a resolution.

[[more:confm|The seven disagreements|C-1 Spray or not. RHS: don't use fungicides. Maryland Extension, an American Rose Society article and the Rose Society of NSW: susceptible roses need regular spraying. C-2 Prevent or react. Some sources spray regularly through the season; others spray only when there is a problem, or "minimally". C-3 How long resistance lasts. RHS: it usually doesn't last. June (NZ Rose Society), July (Ross Roses), a few days after pruning then copper at first spring growth (South Pacific Roses). C-7 How often. Weekly, 7 to 14 days, about 15 days, every two weeks, or two to three weeks; all agree the label decides.]]
