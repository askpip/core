# Founder Review Form — Why Isn't My Bush Rose Flowering?

Fill this in here in the Shed, with the **Decision Brief** open in a second tab. Everything saves as you go and stays private to you until you have both pressed **Finish**. After that you can each see where your answers differ. Either Founder's own finished, decisive answer is enough on its own for the ROC to act.

For each finding, choose one:

**Approve** — a fair and accurate statement of what recognised sources say, at the confidence shown. Put it in Ask Pip's approved knowledge.

**Approve with a change** — yes, but only with the change you describe.

**Flag** — you cannot decide yet: a question, a wording you think is wrong, or you want more evidence. Say which. This is a valid answer.

**Do not approve** — leave it out of Ask Pip's approved knowledge. The research stays on record.

The confidence tag (High, Moderate or Low) is how strong the evidence is. You are approving the finding at that confidence, not changing it.
