## 7. Principal Findings (Assessed Findings)

Findings F1–F10, F12 and F13 are unchanged from `-01` §7 and are not repeated here in full text (see that document). This section states only what changed.

**F1a — strengthened in Round 2.** Further searching across additional US and Australian sources continued to find no use of "bush rose" as a formal classification term outside UK/Commonwealth usage. The observation is unchanged in substance; the evidence behind it is broader.

**F11 — revised in Round 2.** *(Original wording, retained, plus the addition below.)* Watering thoroughly after the first hard frost but before the ground freezes, so the plant enters winter well-hydrated, and clearing fallen leaf debris from around the plant before winter to reduce overwintering fungal spores, were both specifically described as part of winter preparation. **A second independent source (Nebraska Extension, Lancaster County) specifies this more precisely: water to a depth of 8–12 inches, every 2–3 weeks, continuing until the ground freezes even once the plant is dormant, and gives the identical reasoning for debris removal (leaves as a source of overwintering fungal spores).**

## 9. Recommendations for Further Research

Per ROC Operations Manual §7.7 (v2.7): a recommendation is retained here only where resolving it would require exceeding this commission's scope, accepting a different evidentiary standard, or committing materially disproportionate effort — not merely because it had not yet been pursued.

**Resolved in Round 2, no longer open (full reasoning in `FRD-BUSHROSE-BASICCARE-01_Round2_Research_Notes.md`):**

- **Consistency with RECENTPLANT/DORMANCY.** Checked directly against both ARCs' source basis. No conflict. A distinction worth stating for the eventual ARC: RECENTPLANT's "three or more years" concerns pruning readiness specifically; this dossier's "roughly two years" (F2) concerns watering vigilance specifically — compatible, but easily conflated if not stated side by side with their different purposes made explicit.
- **F1's UK/US terminology gap.** Further searching continues to support F1a; folded into F1a above.
- **F11's single-source status.** Resolved; see §7, §8.
- **Q5 depth (F12, F13).** Three further institutional sources reviewed; none offer the general, non-diagnostic framing sought. This is recorded as a checked absence, not a gap in search effort — it supports F12 and F13's existing confidence levels as an accurate reflection of genuinely thin literature.

**Still open — requires a Founder decision (see §10, Decision 2):**

- **Geographic diversity.** All institutional-quality sources in this commission remain from the UK and continental US. Round 2 searched Australian, South African, New Zealand and continental European sources specifically and found the available material in easy reach to be predominantly commercial or enthusiast in nature, not the government/university-extension-equivalent sources this commission has otherwise relied on. Closing this to the same evidentiary standard would require one of: accepting a different (lower, or differently justified) evidentiary bar for these regions; committing to non-English-language sourcing with translation, a materially larger undertaking than the rest of this commission; or direct outreach to a specific candidate institution rather than relying on general search. None of these is a research decision the ROC can make on its own — each trades off against this commission's own established evidentiary standard, which only the Founders can decide to vary.
