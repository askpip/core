[[more:d2m|The options|(a) Four bands as provisionally grouped in this dossier, with fixed boundaries: A zones 3–5, B zone 6, C zones 7–8, D zones 9–10 (Illinois and Pennsylvania statewide months used for zone 6). (b) No bands: months shown only for the gardener's own state (or part of state) where a source exists; season and signals elsewhere. (c) Both: the gardener's own-state months where a source exists; otherwise the months of their band, named with the source they come from; season and signals where neither exists. (d) Defer pending R-1 and R-2. ROC recommendation: (c). It keeps every month tied to the area its source wrote for (AF-6) while still covering gardens in states with no source. Band B would show no pruning month under any option (R-2).]]

[[more:d3m|The options|(a) Ask the gardener for their USDA zone. (b) Derive the zone from the rose's recorded location, using the USDA map (by ZIP code or position). (c) Place by state only.
