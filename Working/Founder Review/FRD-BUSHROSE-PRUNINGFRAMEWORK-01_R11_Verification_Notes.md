## Part 1 — Quotation verification against live sources

**Method.** All 24 sources originally cited in FRD-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-01 §4.1 were re-fetched on 22 September 2026, one day after the original research session, using the WebFetch tool (four parallel passes covering six sources each), with two sources (UCG, and the two Ask Extension pages) followed up individually using a live browser session and direct WebFetch retries respectively after initial fetch difficulties. Every quotation or specific numeric claim attributed to each source in §8 of the dossier was checked against the live page and classified as **VERIFIED** (exact or near-exact match), **PARAPHRASE** (substance supported, wording differs from the quotation marks used), **NOT FOUND** (wording or claim not located on the page), or **PAGE UNAVAILABLE**.

**Overall result:** of roughly 90 individual quotation/claim checks across 24 sources, the overwhelming majority were VERIFIED outright. A handful were fair PARAPHRASES already flagged as such in the original dossier's own text (for example UGA's "the strongest, youngest canes," which the dossier itself labelled "as summarised by the extraction"). Two required correction (KCRS, RBG — both in AF-4). Two sources (AEW, AEO) could not be verified at all and have been removed from the source base. No wrong numbers, reversed advice, or other fabricated quotations were found anywhere else in the dossier.

## Part 2 — Comparison against ARC-BUSHROSE-DORMANCY-01 and ARC-BUSHROSE-RECENTPLANT-01

**Method.** Both ARCs were read in full (not excerpted) and compared, finding by finding, against all 30 Assessed Findings in FRD-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-01/-02.

**ARC-BUSHROSE-DORMANCY-01 (v1.2).** This ARC governs *when* a bush rose is ready to be pruned (bud swell as the readiness signal; climate/hemisphere-dependent timing; mild-climate complications). The pruning-framework dossier governs *how* an already-dormant, already-ready bush is pruned once that gate has been passed — it does not re-address timing or readiness anywhere in its 30 findings. The two commissions are complementary rather than overlapping on any factual point, and no conflict was found. (One surface-level point of contact: both documents discuss "buds," but DORMANCY concerns whether a bud has begun to swell — a readiness signal — while this dossier's AF-21 to AF-24 concern which direction a bud faces and how a cut is placed relative to it. These are different aspects of the same anatomical feature, not a shared claim, and there is nothing to reconcile.)

**ARC-BUSHROSE-RECENTPLANT-01 (v1.0).** This ARC governs *whether* a rose is established enough for full pruning at all (AF-1: full pruning should generally wait roughly three or more years; AF-4: light shaping is acceptable after one full growing season). The pruning-framework dossier is explicitly scoped to "the established bush rose defined in Architecture §5.1" (§2, Scope, Included) and explicitly excludes "any care beyond dormant pruning" outside that established-plant scope. No finding in this dossier contradicts RECENTPLANT's restraint period or its light-shaping allowance. §7.4 of the dossier notes two incidental mentions of young-plant pruning restraint (CLE: first-year plants pruned lightly; RBG: young bushes under three years pruned sparingly) that were correctly left unassessed here, since the recently-planted commission governs them — and on inspection, both statements are consistent with, not contrary to, RECENTPLANT's approved AF-1 and AF-4. Neither CLE nor RBG is currently part of RECENTPLANT's own evidence base, so this is noted as a possible lead for a future RECENTPLANT-strengthening commission, not acted on here.

**One documentation gap noted, not corrected here:** the dossier's §2 Scope explicitly cites `ARC-BUSHROSE-DORMANCY-01` as the basis for its dormant-season scope, but does not similarly cite `ARC-BUSHROSE-RECENTPLANT-01` for its established-plant prerequisite, even though §2's "Related Commissions" field asserts the relevant ARCs "are listed in §1" (they are named in prose in a few places but not formally listed anywhere as a citation list). This is a minor cross-referencing completeness issue, not a substantive one, and is left for a future light-touch pass.

**Conclusion:** no conflict was found between this dossier and either approved ARC. FRD-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-02 §0 records this outcome and marks R-11 resolved.
