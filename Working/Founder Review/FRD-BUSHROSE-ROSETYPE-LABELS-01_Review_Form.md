For each finding, choose one:

**Approve with a change** — yes, but only with the change you describe.

**Do not approve** — leave it out of Ask Pip's approved knowledge. The research stays on record.

The confidence tag (High, Moderate-High, Moderate, Low-Moderate, or Not Assigned) is how strong the evidence is. You are approving the finding at that confidence, not changing it. A "Not Assigned" tag on a negative finding means it records that nothing was found, not that anything is wrong with the finding.

### New Zealand (Brief section 5, group 1)

#### AF-1 — Oderings and Kings Plant Barn populate "Bush Roses" with Hybrid Tea and Floribunda | Confidence: High

> Oderings' "Bush Roses" category is populated specifically with Hybrid Tea and Floribunda cultivars, kept separate from a distinct "Patio Bush" category. Kings Plant Barn names the precise type directly in the product title itself ("...Bush Rose Ht/Fl").
