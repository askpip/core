### Ordinary care across a year (Brief section 5, group 2)

#### F2 — Watering | Confidence: High

> Established bush roses are watered deeply and infrequently, roughly 1–2 inches a week when rainfall is short, with extra care in the first two years.

[[claim:f2|Approve;Approve with a change;Flag;Do not approve]]
[[more:f2m|Backing and caveats|Four independent institutional sources (RHS, Illinois, Missouri, Cornell CCE Rockland), consistent in substance.]]

#### F3 — Growing-season mulching | Confidence: High

> A 2–3 inch layer of organic mulch, kept a few inches clear of the stem, is commonly recommended during the growing season.

[[claim:f3|Approve;Approve with a change;Flag;Do not approve]]
[[more:f3m|Backing and caveats|Four independent institutional sources, consistent; correctly distinguished from the separate winter-mounding practice (F9).]]

#### F4 — Feeding pattern | Confidence: High (pattern) / Not Assigned (any one date)

> Feeding commonly starts in spring and stops by mid-to-late summer so growth can harden before winter. The exact cutoff date varies by region and is not treated as a single fact.

[[claim:f4|Approve;Approve with a change;Flag;Do not approve]]
[[more:f4m|Backing and caveats|Six independent sources agree on the general shape. See Brief section 6 for why the exact date varies by region rather than being one fixed fact.]]

#### F5 — Deadheading pattern | Confidence: High (pattern) / Low (any one date)

> Deadheading is commonly stopped in early-to-mid autumn to let the plant set hips and head toward dormancy. The exact cutoff date varies by region.

[[claim:f5|Approve;Approve with a change;Flag;Do not approve]]
[[more:f5m|Backing and caveats|Five independent sources agree on the general shape; the exact date is regionally variable (Brief section 6).]]

#### F6 — General pest/disease watchfulness | Confidence: High

> Common problems to watch for: blackspot, powdery mildew, aphids, spider mites; water at the base or in the morning to reduce fungal disease.

[[claim:f6|Approve;Approve with a change;Flag;Do not approve]]
[[more:f6m|Backing and caveats|Four independent institutional sources, consistent; a widely and plainly stated category of guidance.]]

### A rose not yet ready for pruning (Brief section 5, group 3)

#### F7 — Minimal cutting on a newly planted rose | Confidence: High

> A rose planted in roughly the past year is generally left largely uncut through its first season — only spent blooms removed, not stems for arrangements — so it can put its energy into establishing.

#### F9 — Soil mounding | Confidence: Very High
