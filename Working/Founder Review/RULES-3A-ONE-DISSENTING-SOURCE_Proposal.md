# Proposal — Pip Knowledge Rules §3A: One Disagreeing Source

**Status:** Proposal for Founder approval. The rules are unchanged until a Founder approves.
**Prepared:** 18 October 2026, by Claude, for the Founders
**Form:** `RULES-3A-ONE-DISSENTING-SOURCE_Proposal_Form.md` (one question)
**Affects:** `Knowledge Curation System/Standards/Pip_Knowledge_Rules.md` §3A, Version 0.5 to 0.6. No published statement changes.

---

## 1. Why

Rule 2 says Pip "says who disagrees". §3A says Pip does not name sources in its sentences, except where the name is the point. Neither says what Pip does when the disagreement is **one source**.

The older-answers check settled it in practice. A Founder's direction of 16 October 2026 (batch 1) led to single dissenting sources being shown as "one source says" or "another says", with the name in the list under the answer. Batch 3 followed the same ruling. A national body is named where the name is the point: for example, "The Royal Horticultural Society advises against them" and "The national rose society says…". This proposal writes that practice into §3A, so later research follows it without asking again.

## 2. The change

In §3A, the **Disagreement (rule 2)** bullet becomes:

> - **Disagreement (rule 2).** The statement says that experts disagree and what each side says. It is marked "Sources disagree" in place of a confidence level. Where one source disagrees, Pip says "one source says" or "another says" and does not name it; its name is in the list under the answer. Pip names it only where the name is the point, as when a national body such as the Royal Horticultural Society disagrees with the rest.

The first two sentences are the current wording. The last two are new.
