## How to fill this in

- **Tick a box** by changing `[ ]` to `[x]`. Tick one box per claim.
- **Comment** by typing after `Comment:`. You need not comment on anything you approve. You must comment on anything you flag, change or do not approve, so your partner can see why.

- **Work alone first.** Compare with your partner only after you have both finished.

**What the four choices mean.** For each claim they stand for the outcomes the ROC Operations Manual (section 11.6) allows: approve; approve subject to amendments; request additional research, correction or clarification, or defer; and decline. Part H records your overall outcome, using all six.

| Choice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | I accept this as a fair and accurate statement of what recognised sources say, at the strength shown, for inclusion in Ask Pip's approved knowledge. |
| **Approve with change** | I accept it, but only with the change I describe in the comment. |
| **Flag** | I cannot decide yet. I have a question, I think the wording is wrong, or I want more evidence first. I say which in the comment. |
| **Do not approve** | Leave this out of Ask Pip's approved knowledge. The research stays in the record. |

**Terms and codes.** Source codes such as OSU or ILL are listed in Brief Appendix A. Terms such as bypass pruners, rootstock sucker, ARC and the six supported observations are in Brief Appendix B.

**Strength** (High, Moderate, Low) is the strength of the evidence for the claim, explained in Brief section 4. You are approving the claim at that strength, not raising or lowering it. If you think a rating is wrong, flag the claim and say so.
