## 2. Why this addendum exists

`ARC-BUSHROSE-ROSETYPE-01` v1.1 AF-L1 (High) records that Kings Plant Barn pairs "Bush" with "a precise type abbreviation" in product titles ("…Bush Rose Ht", "…Bush Rose Fl"). Acting as KIT, Claude proposed a hint for the rose-type question (PKR-SGT-000003) telling gardeners what the abbreviations mean, so that a gardener holding such a label isn't turned away as "bush only". No approved source says what "Ht" and "Fl" stand for, so KIT could not add that meaning itself. Shaphan directed a quick ROC check.

**Question:** Do the abbreviations "Ht" and "Fl" in New Zealand rose product names correspond to Hybrid Tea and Floribunda?

**Method:** Kings Plant Barn product titles found by web search; each cultivar's class then checked against an independent New Zealand nursery that names the class in full (Matthews Roses, Wairere Nursery). Palmers product pages found in the same search were read directly. All pages read on 1 October 2026 through an automated retrieval step; quotations are as that tool returned them and were not checked against the raw page.

## 4. Principal Findings

**AF-1 (established, small sample).** Every Kings Plant Barn rose checked matches the class named in full by an independent New Zealand nursery:

| Kings title | Class named elsewhere | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Frosty Moon Bush Rose Ht" | "Bush Hybrid Tea" | MAT-FM |
| "Legend Bush Rose Ht" | "Hybrid Tea" | WAI-LG |
| "Fordell Bush Rose Fl" | "Bush Floribunda" | MAT-FO |
| "My Daughter Bush Rose Fl" | "Bush Floribunda" | MAT-MD |
| "Summer Sun Bush Rose Fl" | "Bush Floribunda" | MAT-SS |

Five of five match: "Ht" with Hybrid Tea, "Fl" with Floribunda.

**AF-2 (established).** Palmers uses the same abbreviations in its product titles. "My Daughter Bush Fl" is described on the same page as "Floribunda" (PAL-MD). "Fordell Ht 4.7L" is described on its own page as "Floribunda - Exclusive to Matthews Roses" (PAL-FO), and the breeder calls it a Floribunda (MAT-FO), so that title's "Ht" does not match the class. A retailer's abbreviation can therefore be wrong about which included class a rose is, even when both classes are included ones.

**AF-3 (negative finding).** No source located spells out what "Ht" or "Fl" stands for; every page checked uses the abbreviation without expanding it. The meaning in AF-1 is shown by matching, not stated by any retailer.

| Finding | Level | Rationale | Limitations | Remaining uncertainty |

| AF-2 | **Moderate** | Both pages read directly; the mismatch is on the retailer's own page. | Two products from one retailer. | How often Palmers' abbreviations disagree with the class. |
| AF-3 | **Not Assigned** | Negative finding. | Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. | Whether a retailer explains the abbreviations elsewhere (for example on a physical tag). |

---

## 6. Recommendation and Founder Decision

**Recommended default (for KIT):** Pip may tell the gardener that on New Zealand shop labels "Ht" or "HT" usually means Hybrid Tea and "Fl" or "FL" usually means Floribunda, so a label with either counts as a stated type rather than "bush" alone. Because an abbreviation can name the wrong one of the two (AF-2), Pip should say "usually". For the rose-type question this doesn't change the outcome: Hybrid Tea and Floribunda both qualify.

**Founder Decision Point:** Accept AF-1 to AF-3 as stated and the recommended default, for incorporation into `ARC-BUSHROSE-ROSETYPE-01` (as v1.2); accept with amendments; commission a fuller check (for example the Rose Finder once reachable, or more retailers); or decline.
