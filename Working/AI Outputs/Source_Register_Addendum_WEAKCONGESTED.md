## 3. Matters Reported to the Founders (§12.10)

1. **Orange County Rose Society: evidence discrepancy.** AF-5 and §5 C-1 say this source "explicitly questions applying the pencil rule strictly". The page asks "Why... should you consider leaving stems smaller than a pencil?" as a rhetorical set-up, and then argues **against** leaving them. Read correctly, it **supports** the pencil rule. **Effect:** AF-5's core claim (pencil thickness is the near-universal threshold, Moderate) is unaffected, and if anything gains a supporting source. C-1's statement that one source questions the rule's strictness is **not supported by that source**. **Corrected in the ARC at v1.2, at Shaphan's direction.** The approved default ("about the thickness of a pencil") is unchanged either way.
2. **Berkeley Horticultural Nursery (AF-15): not confirmed.** The page found says to skip a pruning season for young roses *other than* Hybrid Teas and Floribundas. That doesn't match the dossier's description, and it excludes the MVP's main rose types. AF-15 (Moderate, young roses get leniency) still rests on Clemson, Illinois and Texas A&M.
3. **Not identified: "NC State" (AF-7 cane count), the photogrammetry literature (AF-9), the "ten causes" source (AF-11). Not confirmed: which Marin Rose Society page (AF-15).** Each affected finding has other confirmed sources. None was substituted.
4. **Minor differences, no effect on findings:** the RSNSW and Iowa State coin page titles; the Patterson abstract wording is paraphrased; the RHS AF-3 quote is on the Rose Dieback page, which isn't in this log.
