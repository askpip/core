# Source Register Addendum — ARC-BUSHROSE-STEMCROSSING-01 and ARC-BUSHROSE-INWARDGROWTH-01 (Approved)

**Prepared by:** ROC, under ROC Operations Manual §12.10 (Version 2.9), at Shaphan's direction, 29 September 2026 ("we need to be able to verify where we have got our information from on every instance").  
**Status:** **Approved by Shaphan, 29 September 2026.** Added to both ARCs as §8 Source Register (version 1.1). KIT has applied it to the Source PKRs (see §4). Founder decision on §3.1: the two timing findings (SC AF-8, IG AF-6) stay at Moderate on two confirmed sources.  
**Scope:** every source in `FRD-BUSHROSE-STEMCROSSING-02` §4 and `FRD-BUSHROSE-INWARDGROWTH-02` §4, plus sources named only in Assessed Finding evidence. **No Assessed Finding, Evidence Confidence Level, conflict, default or Founder decision is changed by this addendum.**

## 1. Method

Each source was located by web search, then opened. It was confirmed as the same document by title, author or organisation, publisher and date (where recorded), and, wherever the dossier quotes it, by finding the quoted passage on the page. All pages were accessed and checked on **29 September 2026**. Where a web address was already held by a Published Source PKR (from the dead-wood or dormancy research), that address is reused and marked "existing record".

## 3. Matters Reported to the Founders (§12.10)

1. **CSU-ASK: Not located.** The Colorado State / Ask Extension answer behind SC AF-8 and IG AF-6 could not be found. **Affected findings:** SC AF-8 and IG AF-6 (both Moderate: deferral is about general timing only). Each still rests on two confirmed independent sources (OSU; ISU-LINN). ROC's view is that neither finding's substance depends on CSU-ASK, but whether the Moderate level stands on two sources rather than three is a Founder judgement. No change is made here.
2. **UAEXFSA: title mis-recorded, and one evidence discrepancy.** The document is "Homeowner's Guide to Pruning Ornamental Trees", not "Pruning Landscape Trees and Shrubs". The dossier said it applies the removal cut to crossing branches specifically. It defines the removal cut and separately lists crossing branches as a problem, but doesn't connect them. **Affected finding:** SC AF-6 (Moderate: full cut to point of origin). It still has five other confirmed sources (KCRS, ARSTEN, PRS, MOG, and USDAFS as general concurrence). No change is made here.
3. **Minor recording differences, no effect on findings:** RHS shrub-roses wording (same meaning, not verbatim); UFIFAS recorded under a descriptive title; ARSTEN's page spells "Principals"; no date shown on the Yates pruning page.
