**Prepared by:** ROC, under ROC Operations Manual §12.10 (Version 2.9), 29 September 2026.  
**Status:** **Approved by Shaphan, 29 September 2026.** Added to the ARC as §8 Source Register (version 1.1).  
**Scope:** every source in `FRD-BUSHROSE-DAMAGEDGROWTH-02` §4 (52 rows; the five Ask Extension cases are listed separately), plus one source named only in Assessed Finding evidence. **No Assessed Finding, Evidence Confidence Level, conflict, default or Founder decision is changed.**

## 1. Method

The same method as the crossing/inward register: each source was located, opened, and matched by title, author or organisation, publisher and date, and by finding the quoted passage wherever the dossier quotes one. All checks were made on 29 September 2026. "Existing record" means the web address was already held by a Published Source PKR. "Located (title match only)" means the page couldn't be opened (access blocked) but its exact title was found at that address.
