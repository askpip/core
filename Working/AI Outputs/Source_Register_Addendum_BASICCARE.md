# Source Register Addendum — ARC-BUSHROSE-BASICCARE-01 (Approved)

**Prepared by:** ROC, under ROC Operations Manual v2.9 §12.6 and §12.10, 29 September 2026.  
**Status:** Approved by Shaphan in chat, 29 September 2026 (decisions: 1 yes, 2 yes, 3 no). Incorporated as `ARC-BUSHROSE-BASICCARE-01` §8 Source Register (version 1.2).

## 1. Method

Each address was opened on 29 September 2026. A source is "Confirmed" where the title matches and the passage behind the finding was found. The Round 2 sources reviewed and not used as evidence (UC IPM, University of Tennessee, University of Arizona, and the non-US/UK sources in the Round 2 Research Notes) aren't listed, because no finding cites them.

## 3. Matters Reported to the Founders (§12.10)

1. **The RHS rose-care page has moved.** The dossier's RHS source for F2 and F3 (advice profile PID=191) now redirects to an unrelated page. The RHS's current page, "How to grow roses", states the same substance: a weekly soak rather than light, frequent watering; regular watering in the first few years; mulch at least 2 inches thick, kept clear of the stems. **Decided (D1): RHS-GROW adopted** as the RHS source for F2 and F3. F2 and F3 are High on four other confirmed sources either way.
2. **F13 is attributed to the wrong source.** The ARC names Missouri G6601, but that page doesn't make F13's claim. The claim is in the University of Maryland page already in the source log (UMD-PROB): "Poor growing conditions, incorrect care, weather extremes, and soggy soil are the major cause of plant decline", and most problems aren't disease or insects "especially if the plant has been in your landscape for less than two years". **Decided (D2): F13's source corrected to UMD-PROB.** The finding's wording and its Low–Moderate level are unchanged; it is still a single-source claim.
3. **Source lists that don't match the dossier.** For F2 and F3, the ARC names Iowa State where the dossier's §8 names Illinois. Both pages support both findings (Iowa's mulch figure is 2–4 inches). For F4 and F5, the ARC names three sources where the dossier counts six and five. For F6, the ARC names two sources plus "two further sources" without naming them. **Decided (D2), as proposed:**
   - list both Iowa State and Illinois for F2 and F3;
   - name Iowa State as a confirmed F6 source (it lists all four problems and says to water the soil, and in the morning if overhead);
   - leave the F4/F5 lists as the ARC names them, recording here that Iowa State, Missouri, RHS and Cornell also state the feeding pattern.
   No confidence level changes.
4. **Figures that differ, with no change to findings:** Cobb County mounds 12–18 inches and Nebraska 8–10 inches, against F9's approximately 10–12. Cornell gives 2 inches of mulch and Iowa 2–4, against F3's 2–3. The Penn State page wasn't found to describe a collar or burlap wrap (F10 still rests on UNH, which describes both).
5. **Fine Gardening** is written by Paul Zimmerman himself, not by someone citing him.
