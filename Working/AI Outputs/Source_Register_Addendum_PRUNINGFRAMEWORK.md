# Source Register Addendum — ARC-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-01 (Approved)

**Prepared by:** ROC, under ROC Operations Manual §12.6/§12.10 (Version 2.9), 29 September 2026.  
**Status:** **Approved by Shaphan, 29 September 2026.** Added to the ARC as §8 Source Register (version 1.1).  
**Scope:** all 28 sources in `FRD-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-04` §4.1, which already recorded web addresses, plus the sources behind AF-31 to AF-41. Those were named in the Assessed Finding evidence but had no addresses, so they were located afresh. **No Assessed Finding, Evidence Confidence Level, conflict, default or Founder decision is changed.**

## 1. Method

Where the dossier gave a web address, the address was opened on 29 September 2026 and matched by title and author. "Existing record" means the same address was already opened and confirmed on 29 September 2026 for another register, or is held by a Published Source PKR. For AF-31 to AF-41's sources, each was located and matched by title, author and date, and by finding the quoted passage where the dossier quotes one.
