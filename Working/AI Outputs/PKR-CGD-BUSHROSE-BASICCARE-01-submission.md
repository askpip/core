## 7. Founder Review Rendering (plain summary)

When the pruning journey stops because the rose isn't dormant or is too newly planted, Pip explains why and then offers care instead.

- **Growing season:**
  - water deeply about once a week in dry spells;
  - mulch 2–3 inches deep, clear of the stems;
  - feed from spring to mid-to-late summer;
  - deadhead until early-to-mid autumn;
  - watch for blackspot, mildew, aphids and mites, and water at the base;
  - notice early signs;
  - check growing conditions first.
- **Recently planted:** in the first year, leave the leaves and stems and only remove faded flowers. Water carefully for about two years. Pruning waits about three years.
- **Winter, where frosts are hard:**
  - after several hard frosts, mound 10–12 inches around the base, and remove the mound in spring;
  - in exposed spots, add a collar or wrap;
  - water well before the ground freezes;
  - clear fallen leaves.

Every item carries its own confidence and sources. There are no dates. A note says the advice comes mainly from UK and US sources.
