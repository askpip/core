## 4. Decisions for the Founders

**Decision 1 — Where the check lives.** The approved post-bud-break "removal only" session excludes shortening healthy canes (`ARC-BUSHROSE-POSTBUDBREAK-01` §2 D2), and blind shoots can't be seen in the winter pruning walk-through.
- (a) A separate growing-season check on the rose's page and in the "Why isn't my rose flowering?" answer, outside the dormancy gate. *KIT recommendation.*
- (b) Add it to the removal-only session, changing that session's approved condition.

**Decision 2 — When Pip offers it.** No source says how soon a shoot without a bud counts as blind.
- (a) Offer it only when the gardener says other shoots on the rose already have buds or flowers. *KIT recommendation, as an approved default.*
- (b) Offer it at any time in the growing season.

**Decision 3 — Recently planted roses (PKR-SGT-000002).**
- (a) The recently-planted gate applies, as it does to other cuts. *KIT recommendation.*
- (b) It doesn't apply to this check.

## 5. Wording Boundary Check

Every statement restates AF-21, AF-22 or AF-24. "This should give a new shoot that can flower later in the season" restates the RHS wording quoted in AF-24. No cause is named to the gardener. The "Doesn't match" note ("Leave it to flower") states no horticultural claim beyond AF-21's definition.

## 6. Founder Review Rendering (plain summary)

In the growing season, Pip can walk a gardener through blind shoots: shoots with healthy leaves but no flower bud at the tip (strong evidence). Pip says nobody knows the cause, and won't confirm one from a photo. Once the gardener confirms there's no bud, they can cut it back by about half to a strong bud (the RHS way), leave it, decide later, or get help. Pip mentions that other sources cut to the next leaf joint or the next full five-leaflet leaf. If the gardener isn't sure, Cut isn't offered. Building it needs app work: a new growing-season check, outside the winter pruning walk-through.
