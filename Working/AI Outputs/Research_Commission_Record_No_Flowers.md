## 3. Background — the gap

Approved knowledge touches flowering only indirectly. `ARC-BUSHROSE-BASICCARE-01` F12 lists "reduced flowering" among general signs worth noticing before diagnosis, and F13 (Low–Moderate) records that ordinary growing conditions are the most common cause of decline. `ARC-BUSHROSE-PRUNINGFRAMEWORK-01` AF-1 gives encouraging flowering as a purpose of pruning, and AF-19 records that once-flowering roses are pruned differently. No approved finding says why an established bush rose flowers poorly or not at all, what a gardener can see that separates one cause from another, or what to do. Pip therefore cannot answer the question.

## 4. Commissioned Questions

1. **Q1.** What causes do credible sources give for an established bush rose (Hybrid Tea, Floribunda, provisionally Grandiflora) producing few or no flowers? For example: too little sun, pruning (timing or severity), too much nitrogen, water stress, age or establishment, disease and pests, competition from tree roots, blind shoots, buds that fail to open (balling), rootstock taking over, variety habit (once-flowering), and weather.
2. **Q2.** What can a beginner observe that sources say distinguishes one cause from another?
3. **Q3.** What do sources advise doing for each cause?
4. **Q4.** Where do sources disagree, and what remains unanswered?

## 5. Scope and Method

Established bush roses within the approved scope (Architecture §5.1). Newly planted roses and once-flowering roses are outside the scope of guidance but are researched here to the extent sources give them as explanations a beginner may meet. Standard ROC method, with United Kingdom, United States, Australian and New Zealand institutional sources where available; commercial sources allowed and labelled. **Every online source must be recorded with its web address and access date (ROC OM §5.5),** and the ARC must carry a Source Register (§12.6). Findings keep strictly to what sources say; the ROC gives no horticultural advice of its own.

## 6. Expected Deliverables

`FRD-BUSHROSE-NOFLOWERS-01`, with per-finding Evidence Confidence Levels and Founder Decision Points; a plain-language Decision Brief; and `Source_Register_NOFLOWERS.json`.

## 7. Interim Behaviour

Until this commission's findings are approved, Pip doesn't diagnose why a rose is not flowering. If a gardener asks, Pip may point to the approved general signs in `ARC-BUSHROSE-BASICCARE-01` F12 and suggest experienced local help.
