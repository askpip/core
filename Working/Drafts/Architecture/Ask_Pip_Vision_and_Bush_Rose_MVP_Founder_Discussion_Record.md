# 3. First MVP Scope

## 3.1 Supported Rose Scope and Assisted Photo Interpretation

**Founder decision:** The first journey supports an established bush rose that appears suitable for locally appropriate pruning, is safely accessible and is not evidently outside approved boundaries because of recent planting, severe stress, major damage, significant disease, unsuitable timing or another unsupported condition.

The gardener may provide contextual information and photographs. Pip may use an authorised vision capability to identify possible relevant features and compare them with approved reference material. Pip presents interpretations as proposed observations with uncertainty, not as established facts, diagnoses or autonomous pruning decisions.

The gardener examines the actual rose and confirms the relevant condition. Inadequate photographs, unresolved interpretation, inability to confirm or an unsupported situation require better evidence, deferral or experienced local help.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 3.2 Supported Observations

**Founder decision:** Include:

- dead versus living wood;
- damaged growth;
- crossing or rubbing stems;
- inward-growing stems;
- weak or congested growth; and
- the main framework to retain.

Pip may propose these from photographs; the gardener confirms them on the physical rose.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 3.3 Supported Choices

**Founder decision:** Support **Cut**, **Leave**, **Decide later** and **Get experienced local help**. Pip may propose a plan from assisted photo interpretation, gardener-confirmed observations and approved rules. Before every cut, the gardener confirms the intended stem on the physical rose. Identification, timing, safety or knowledge uncertainty requires deferral.

**Decision status:** Resolved

# 4. Comparison Material

## 4.1 Required Material

**Founder decision:** Every supported observation requires at least one approved beginner-appropriate comparison. Each reference identifies the feature demonstrated, viewpoint and visible context, relevant rose and seasonal context, common lookalikes, limitations, source, verification, version and approval status. The session retains a reference to the exact comparison used.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 4.2 Presentation and Accessibility

**Founder decision:** Require clear overview and close-up images; highlighting or annotation; plain-language explanation; accessible labels and alternative text; common lookalikes where relevant; an explanation of what the image cannot establish; and an explicit distinction between the gardener's image, Pip's proposed interpretation and the approved reference.

**Decision status:** Resolved

# 5. Suitability and Safety

**Founder decision:** Required decision areas are supported rose type; location and seasonal context; dormancy or active growth; recent planting; visible stress, damage or disease; clean and sharp secateurs; suitable personal protection; safe access and working conditions; and adequate photographs and confidence in the confirmed observation.

Exact questions, acceptable answers and stopping thresholds shall come from approved horticultural and safety knowledge.

**Decision status:** Resolved

# 6. Follow-Up Design

## 6.1 Timing

**Founder decision:** Follow-up is event-based and locally adjustable. Pip offers a provisional window using the pruning date, location, season, weather context and approved rules. The gardener may accept, change or decline it.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 6.2 Observations

**Founder decision:** Request comparable overview and close-up photographs and assess new shoots or buds, apparent dieback or damage, overall structure and response, unexpected symptoms and whether earlier expectations were met. Pip may compare new and saved photographs with approved references while expressing uncertainty and seeking confirmation where needed.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 6.3 Outcome Questions

**Founder decision:** Ask:

1. Did the pruning journey help you understand your rose better?
2. Did comparing photographs and examples help you recognise what you were seeing?
3. Was it useful that Pip remembered the earlier session and photographs?
4. Would you return with this rose, another plant or another gardening question?

**Decision status:** Resolved

# 7. Prototype Validation

## 7.1 Measures

**Founder decision:** Measure journey completion; confirmed, corrected and unresolved photo proposals; identification of relevant structures; understanding; appropriate use of all four choices; safety deferrals and requests for better evidence; usefulness of comparisons, automatic history and remembered context; willingness to return; and preference against written guidance or a general AI conversation.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 7.2 Success Thresholds

**Founder decision:** Do not invent fixed percentages. Horticultural and safety experts first define unacceptable errors. Early moderated testing establishes baseline results. Numerical thresholds are then set from evidence before broader trials. Every unsafe recommendation, unsupported certainty or failure to defer is reviewed individually. Photo interpretation is measured separately from the gardener-confirmed final decision.

**Decision status:** Resolved

# 8. Alignment and Knowledge

## 8.1 Shared Product Boundary

**Founder decision:** Both MVP documents shall describe:

> A guided bush-rose pruning journey using assisted photo interpretation, approved comparison material, gardener confirmation, governed horticultural rules, automatic plant history and one meaningful follow-up. Pip may propose observations but does not independently diagnose the rose or authorise a cut from an image alone.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 8.2 Vision Alignment

**Founder decision:** The MVP supports Ask Pip's vision by helping the gardener learn about an individual plant, preserving that plant's story and remembering it at follow-up.

**Decision status:** Resolved

## 8.3 Trusted Knowledge

**Founder decision:** Before gardener testing, approved knowledge must cover bush-rose pruning and supported observations; timing and local suitability; stress, damage, disease and deferral conditions; tool and personal safety; approved comparisons and lookalikes; uncertainty; and experienced-help conditions. Horticultural experts develop and verify this material. The Founders approve it through the governed knowledge process before operational use.

**Decision status:** Resolved
