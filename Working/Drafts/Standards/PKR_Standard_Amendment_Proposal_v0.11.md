# 1. What This Proposes

Two changes to the PKR Standard, and one correction that the second change depends on.

1. **Question answers.** A Care Guidance PKR may be presented as the answer to a common question a gardener opens, as well as at a point in the journey.
2. **Growing-season checks.** The Standard names a second journey context beside the dormant pruning journey: a short, separate check on one observation while the rose is in growth.
3. **Supported observations.** The Standard says an Observation PKR is "one of the six supported observations". Eight are Published. The wording is corrected so that an approved observation is a supported one.

The Standard stays at v0.10 and stays authoritative until a Founder approves this amendment and it is integrated.

# 2. Why

**Question answers.** On 2 October 2026 a Founder approved PKR-CGD-000007 ("Can I kill my rose by pruning too hard?") and PKR-CGD-000008 ("Why isn't my rose flowering?") as Advisory care guidance, with "when the gardener opens this question" as the presentation point (Decision 1(a) of that package). KIT was to propose the amendment that names this in the Standard. Section 5.7 today describes an Advisory as a caution "alongside the journey" and a Presentation Point as a journey point identified by PKR.

**Growing-season checks.** On the same day a Founder approved PKR-OBS-000008 and PKR-DEC-000008 (blind shoots) as a separate check outside the pruning journey and its dormancy gate (Decision 1(a) of that package). The Standard's scope today is "established bush rose, dormant pruning", and it has no words for a check that runs in the growing season.

**Supported observations.** Sections 3 and 5.1 limit an Observation PKR to six named observations. PKR-OBS-000007 (rootstock suckers) and PKR-OBS-000008 (blind shoots) were approved and Published without the Standard being updated.

# 3. Proposed Wording

Words added or changed are in **bold**. Text not shown is unchanged.

## Change 1 — §2 Scope, first paragraph

**Current:** This Standard is scoped first to what the approved Bush Rose V1 Architecture requires: the six supported observations, their comparison material, their decision logic and their supporting sources, and the care guidance that accompanies them (how an approved action is carried out, general cautions shown alongside the journey, and care for a rose while pruning isn't appropriate).

**Proposed:** This Standard is scoped first to what the approved Bush Rose V1 Architecture requires: the **supported observations**, their comparison material, their decision logic and their supporting sources, and the care guidance that accompanies them (how an approved action is carried out, general cautions shown alongside the journey, **answers to common questions,** and care for a rose while pruning isn't appropriate). **It covers the dormant pruning journey and growing-season checks (§4.4).**

## Change 2 — §3 PKR Types Currently Required

**Observation PKR row, current:** One of the six supported observations (dead vs. living wood, damaged growth, crossing/rubbing stems, inward-growing stems, weak/congested growth, main framework to retain)

**Observation PKR row, proposed:** **One supported observation. The Architecture names six for the dormant pruning journey** (dead vs. living wood, damaged growth, crossing/rubbing stems, inward-growing stems, weak/congested growth, main framework to retain). **The Founders have since approved two more: rootstock suckers, and blind shoots (a growing-season check, §4.4). A further observation becomes supported when the Founders approve its PKR package.**

**Care Guidance PKR row, current:** … a general caution shown alongside the journey (for example, how much of a bush to remove in one session), or care for a rose while pruning isn't appropriate. …

**Care Guidance PKR row, proposed:** … a general caution shown alongside the journey (for example, how much of a bush to remove in one session), **the answer to a common question a gardener opens,** or care for a rose while pruning isn't appropriate. …

## Change 3 — §4 Common Fields, Applies To

**Current:** **Applies To** — the plant type, and where relevant the growth stage or season, this record is scoped to (the MVP scope is: established bush rose, dormant pruning);

**Proposed:** **Applies To** — the plant type, and where relevant the growth stage, season **or journey context (§4.4)**, this record is scoped to (the MVP scope is: established bush rose; **dormant pruning, or a growing-season check**);

## Change 4 — new §4.4 Journey Context

**Proposed new section, placed after §4.3:**

> ## 4.4 Journey Context
>
> A journey context is the setting in which Pip presents an Observation PKR and its Decision Logic PKR. Two are defined:
>
> - **Dormant pruning journey** — the guided pruning session governed by the Suitability Gate PKRs, including the removal-only session the dormancy gate allows once a rose is in growth.
> - **Growing-season check** — a short, separate check on one observation that can be seen only while the rose is in growth. It runs outside the dormant pruning journey, and the dormancy gate does not govern it.
>
> A record's journey context is stated in Applies To. An Observation or Decision Logic PKR that states none belongs to the dormant pruning journey.
>
> For a growing-season check:
>
> - its Observation PKR and Decision Logic PKR are built, reviewed and published as for any other observation, and every requirement of §5.1 and §5.3 applies unchanged, including the Confirmation Requirement and the closed Confirmation Responses;
> - Pip shall not present its observation inside the dormant pruning journey, or a dormant-pruning observation inside the check;
> - its Decision Logic PKR shall state, for each Suitability Gate PKR, whether that gate applies to the check (§5.3);
> - where the observation can be judged only once the rose has reached a certain stage, its Observation PKR shall carry an Offer Condition (§5.1).
>
> A further journey context shall be added through Founder approval of an amendment to this Standard, not assumed by analogy.

## Change 5 — §5.1 Observation PKR

**Observation Name, current:** **Observation Name** — one of the six supported observations;

**Observation Name, proposed:** **Observation Name** — **a supported observation (§3)**;

**New field, added at the end of the list:**

> - **Offer Condition** (growing-season checks only, where §4.4 requires it) — a closed question Pip asks before presenting the observation, the answer that lets the check proceed, and the note shown for every other answer. It is stated declaratively (§9.1). It traces to an Assessed Finding, or to an approved practical default labelled plainly as a default. It decides only whether the observation can be judged yet; it is not a Suitability Gate.

## Change 6 — §5.3 Decision Logic PKR, Conditions

**Current:** **Conditions** — what must be true (including any Suitability Gate PKRs) for each choice to be horticulturally acceptable;

**Proposed:** **Conditions** — what must be true (including any Suitability Gate PKRs) for each choice to be horticulturally acceptable. **For a growing-season check, Conditions shall state, for each Suitability Gate PKR, whether it applies to the check (§4.4);**

## Change 7 — §5.7 Care Guidance PKR

**Advisory mode, current:** **Advisory** — a general caution Pip presents alongside the journey at stated points (for example, not removing more than about a third of a bush in one session). An Advisory informs; it never blocks, stops or reorders the journey;

**Advisory mode, proposed:** **Advisory** — **general guidance Pip presents at stated points: a caution alongside the journey** (for example, not removing more than about a third of a bush in one session)**, or the answer to a common question a gardener opens (for example, why a rose isn't flowering)**. An Advisory informs; it never blocks, stops or reorders the journey;

**Presentation Points, current:** **Presentation Points** — when Pip presents this guidance, stated declaratively as journey points identified by PKR (for example, "when the gardener chooses Cut under PKR-DEC-000002", or "when PKR-SGT-000001 stops the journey"). Where an Advisory repeats, the interval is stated as a plain value (for example, "before the first cut of a session, then after every third cut"), per §9.1. A Presentation Point shall never be a condition for proceeding;

**Presentation Points, proposed:**

> - **Presentation Points** — when Pip presents this guidance, stated declaratively. **A Presentation Point is one of:**
>   - **a journey point,** identified by PKR (for example, "when the gardener chooses Cut under PKR-DEC-000002", or "when PKR-SGT-000001 stops the journey"). Where an Advisory repeats, the interval is stated as a plain value (for example, "before the first cut of a session, then after every third cut"), per §9.1; **or**
>   - **a question answer: when the gardener opens a named common question (for example, "when the gardener opens 'Why isn't my rose flowering?'"). The question is one of a closed list the app offers; a gardener does not type it. The record names the question by what it asks. The wording shown on screen is app copy.**
>
>   **A record may have Presentation Points of both kinds.** A Presentation Point shall never be a condition for proceeding;

**New paragraph, placed after the Boundary with Decision Logic and Suitability Gate PKRs:**

> **Question answers.** A question answer is information only. Opening one shall not start, pass or change a journey, a gate or a choice. It may offer a way into a journey or a growing-season check that is governed by its own Observation and Decision Logic PKRs. The boundary above applies to a question answer as it does to all other Care Guidance.

## Change 8 — new §4.5 Reuse of Published Statements in a Question Answer (see Decision 1)

**Proposed new section, placed after §4.4:**

> ## 4.5 Reuse of Published Statements in a Question Answer
>
> The app may answer a common question by showing statements from Published PKRs of any type, word for word, each with the Evidence Confidence, sources and limitations its record carries for it. Such reuse adds no content and needs no new record. It shall not present a choice, and shall not stand in for a gate or a confirmation.

# 4. Effect on Published Records and the App

No Published record needs revising. KIT checked the Published records against the proposed wording:

| Record | What the proposed wording requires | What the record already carries |
|---|---|---|
| PKR-CGD-000007, PKR-CGD-000008 | A question-answer Presentation Point | "When the gardener opens …" as the Presentation Point; Applies To ends "answer to a common question" |
| PKR-OBS-000008 | Journey context in Applies To; an Offer Condition | Applies To ends "in the growing season"; the offer question, its answers and its "come back later" note |
| PKR-DEC-000008 | Whether each Suitability Gate applies | Rose-type gate applies; recently-planted gate applies; dormancy gate does not apply |
| PKR-OBS-000001 to 000007 and their Decision Logic PKRs | Nothing new | None is marked as a growing-season check, so all stay in the dormant pruning journey |

The app needs no change. It already keeps the blind-shoot observation out of the pruning journey and shows the two answers only when their questions are opened.

# 5. Decisions for the Founders

**Decision 1 — Reuse of Published statements (Change 8).** Thirteen of the app's common questions are answered with statements from records built for the journey (a gate, two observations and five Care Guidance records), shown outside those records' own Presentation Points. The Standard says nothing about this.

- (a) Include Change 8, so the Standard permits it and sets its limits. *KIT recommendation.*
- (b) Leave Change 8 out. The practice continues under the app-wording approval, without a rule in the Standard.

**Decision 2 — How the Standard names supported observations (Changes 1, 2 and 5).**

- (a) As drafted: the six from the Architecture, the two approved since, and a rule that a further observation becomes supported when the Founders approve its package. *KIT recommendation.*
- (b) List all eight by name and require an amendment to this Standard for each new one.

**Decision 3 — Approval and version.** Approve the amendment, with the choices above, as PKR Standard **v0.11**.

KIT applies the approved wording to `Knowledge Curation System/Standards/PKR_Standard.md`, updates its Version, Last Updated and Revision Note, and records the change in `CHANGELOG.md`. No record is republished.
