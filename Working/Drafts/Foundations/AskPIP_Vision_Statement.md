## Our Vision

Ask Pip exists to help gardeners grow in their understanding of each individual plant through its unique story, so they can care for it with confidence.

Pip is the friendly companion through whom that purpose becomes personal. Pip helps the gardener observe, understand, decide and return to what happened, while remembering the history the gardener has chosen to record about each plant.

Whether a gardener is pruning a rose, diagnosing an apple tree, nurturing a vegetable garden or caring for a treasured specimen, Ask Pip exists to help them understand the individual plant before them.

## Why Ask Pip Exists

Plants are living organisms. Each develops its own story, shaped by its species, environment, seasons, care and experiences.

Gardeners have access to an abundance of horticultural information, but applying general knowledge to an individual plant can be difficult. General advice cannot fully account for every plant's history, condition and circumstances.

Ask Pip exists to bridge that gap.

By combining trusted horticultural knowledge with the developing story of each plant, Ask Pip helps gardeners make informed decisions based on established knowledge and the evidence available from the plant in their care.

## What the Vision Means

Ask Pip seeks to understand each plant as an individual.

Pip helps gardeners build confidence through knowledge, observation and experience. Guidance should be trustworthy, transparent and evidence-based, with uncertainty communicated honestly.

Useful interactions should create the plant's continuing record naturally. Photographs, observations, questions, decisions, actions and outcomes can become part of its story without asking the gardener to maintain a separate journal as an additional chore.

When the gardener returns, Pip should use that recorded history to continue the relationship: remembering what was observed and decided, asking what happened next and helping the gardener learn from change over time.

Pip does not replace the gardener's judgement. Pip strengthens it by helping the gardener understand what they are seeing, why it matters and how that understanding can inform their care.

## Our Long-Term Vision

As Ask Pip grows, it may support an expanding range of plants, environments and gardening challenges.

Its technology may evolve, its governed knowledge may expand and its methods of observing and understanding plants may improve. The history of an individual gardener's plants remains distinct from general horticultural knowledge. A gardener's records do not automatically become approved platform knowledge.

Throughout that development, guidance should be informed by trusted horticultural knowledge and, as it develops, the unique recorded story of the individual plant.

We envision gardeners becoming less dependent on conflicting generic advice and more able to approach each plant with understanding.

The gardener, the garden and Pip can grow in understanding together. Each season can add to the plant's story. Each observation can deepen understanding. Each informed decision can build the gardener's confidence.

## Our Commitment

Every Ask Pip product, feature, research project and technical decision should support this vision.

Ask Pip will continue developing ways to help gardeners understand and care for the plants entrusted to them while remaining grounded in its enduring purpose:

> Ask Pip exists to help gardeners grow in their understanding of each individual plant through its unique story, so they can care for it with confidence.
