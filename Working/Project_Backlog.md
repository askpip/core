## How to use this list

- **Before starting new work** (a research commission, a PKR build, a Standard revision), check the relevant section below for open items that should be picked up alongside it, rather than creating a fresh one that duplicates something already flagged.
- **When new work surfaces a gap** it doesn't resolve, add it here rather than only mentioning it in conversation — a flag that lives only in a chat message is easy to lose.
- **When an item is resolved**, move it to "Resolved" at the bottom with the date and the document that resolved it, rather than deleting it — this list is also a quick history of what's been caught and fixed.
- Each item names the document(s) it traces back to, so context isn't lost even if the item sits untouched for a while.

## Research Candidates for "The Other Five" (from Drive Migration, 9 September 2026)

Raw material extracted from two Google Drive research documents during the founding-material migration, kept specifically because it's usable input for **"The other five MVP pruning observations"** above (damaged growth, crossing/rubbing stems, inward-growing stems, weak/congested growth, main framework) — not yet formal findings, not yet sourced to Evidence Assessment Standard rigor, just candidate leads for whoever researches these next. The two source documents themselves were judged not worth migrating in full (see note at end) — this is the only part of them kept.

**From "AskPIP MVP Bush Rose Knowledge Pack" (Drive, 30 July 2026, an MVP AI-validation exercise — its "claim levels" taxonomy and mandated 7-section AI output format do NOT reflect the shipped design and should not be reused; the shipped dead-wood feature uses a plainer per-signal confidence rating instead, see `App/src/data/confidenceDefinitions.ts` and `App/supabase/functions/pip-observe-dead-wood/index.ts`):**

- [ ] **Crossing/rubbing stems** — "overlapping stems in a two-dimensional image cannot always be distinguished from stems that physically cross or touch." A ready-made photo-request pattern: ask the gardener for a second image from roughly 90 degrees to the first, to show whether stems actually touch or only overlap in that view.
- [ ] **Main framework to retain** — candidate identification rule: a main/framework stem is defined by *structural role*, not thickness alone. A vigorous new basal shoot may be thick but not yet a mature framework stem; a smaller older stem may still be part of the framework.
- [ ] **Weak/congested growth** — candidate caveat: vigour is relative to the individual plant and photo context, not an absolute size; apparent stem thickness can be distorted by camera perspective.
- [ ] **Damaged growth** — candidate non-determinable list for pruning-wound-type damage: cause, date, tool used, and whether discoloration is superficial or internal are not reliably assessable from a photograph alone.
- [ ] **Inward-growing stems** — the source document has essentially no dedicated treatment of this observation; nothing usable was found. Flagging the gap rather than the absence of a lead.

**From "FRD Commission 008 – Bush Rose User Needs Analysis" (Drive, ~22 July 2026, ROC's full 100KB research record — judged fully superseded/absorbed by the Approved Architecture and Journey documents, so only this extract was kept, not the document itself):**

- [ ] **Raw user-question catalogue** (~40 specific phrased gardener questions across categories including flowering, watering, nutrition, pests, containers and transplanting, each with a frequency/experience-level rationale) — outside current MVP scope entirely, but potential seed material if/when PIP expands past the six bounded pruning observations.
- [ ] **Candidate anatomy/vocabulary list** (cane, basal shoot, sucker, bud eye, lateral shoot, crown, graft union, plus growth-stage terms) — a non-authoritative starting glossary, offered as a head start for whichever future Knowledge Record research needs controlled terminology, not as settled definitions.

**Confirmed not migrated, for the record:** the Founder Executive Brief and full FRD for Commission 008 (findings fully subsumed by the Approved Architecture's Product Principles); the two Volume VII "Bush Rose Pruning Assistant" User Journey and Screen & Interaction Specification documents (confirmed superseded — different screen flow, no profile-creation step, a two-button Complete/Skip choice set instead of the shipped four choices, no stated six-observation taxonomy; their one distinct idea, a richer "Garden Journal" concept than the MVP's session history, is noted here as a possible future-enhancement idea, not a migration candidate); the "AskPIP AI Validation Prompt v1" (only meaningful alongside the superseded Knowledge Pack format it was written for).
