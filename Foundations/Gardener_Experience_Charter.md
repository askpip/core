## Purpose

This charter defines the experience PIP exists to create for every gardener.

It establishes the principles that guide every future product, research, design and technical decision. As PIP grows to support new plants, new technologies and new capabilities, the experience described in this charter shall remain constant.

## Introduction

**We believe every gardener deserves trustworthy guidance that relates to the unique plants growing in their own garden.**

Plants are living organisms. Every one develops differently, shaped by its species, environment, history and care.

While horticultural knowledge is widely available, applying that knowledge to an individual plant is often difficult. Generic advice cannot fully account for the unique story of every plant or the circumstances of every gardener.

PIP exists to bridge that gap.

By combining trusted horticultural knowledge with the history and observations of individual plants, PIP helps gardeners make informed decisions with growing understanding and confidence.

Everything within PIP exists to fulfil this purpose.

## 1. Who PIP Exists For

PIP exists for anyone who wishes to understand and care for plants more confidently.

Whether someone is tending a single rose bush, managing an orchard, growing vegetables for their family or caring for a lifetime collection of ornamental plants, every gardener eventually encounters situations where knowledge alone is not enough.

Questions arise. Conditions change. Plants respond differently than expected. Uncertainty replaces confidence.

PIP exists for those moments.

Its purpose is to help gardeners understand what they are observing, evaluate the available evidence and make informed decisions about the plants entrusted to their care.

## 2. The Problem PIP Exists To Solve

The challenge facing gardeners is rarely a lack of information.

The challenge is applying trusted horticultural knowledge to a unique living plant.

Every plant has developed differently. Every garden provides different growing conditions. Every season adds another chapter to a plant's history. Every gardener has different goals, experience and priorities.

No diagram, article or video can fully account for those differences.

PIP exists to transform trusted horticultural knowledge into guidance that relates to the individual plant standing before the gardener.

## 3. The Experience PIP Exists To Create

PIP is more than a source of information. It is a trusted gardening companion.

Every interaction should help gardeners move from uncertainty towards understanding.

PIP seeks to understand the gardener's situation by considering: the individual plant; its species and characteristics; its recorded history; current observations; photographs where available; the gardener's goals; the available evidence.

Using Founder-approved, evidence-based horticultural knowledge, PIP provides the most personalised guidance that can honestly be supported.

PIP explains its reasoning. PIP communicates its confidence. PIP acknowledges uncertainty. PIP teaches as well as advises.

The objective is not simply to answer today's question but to help gardeners become more capable tomorrow.

## 4. Every Plant Has Its Own Story

No two plants share exactly the same journey.

Even plants of the same species, planted on the same day and growing side by side, will develop differently over time.

For this reason, PIP treats every plant as an individual.

From the moment a gardener introduces a plant to PIP, its story begins. Photographs. Observations. Questions. Care provided. Pruning history. Flowering. Fruiting. Health events. Seasonal development. Successes. Challenges.

Together these form the continuing story of that plant.

As the story grows, so too does PIP's understanding. Future guidance is informed not only by trusted horticultural knowledge but also by the accumulated history of that individual plant.

## 5. What Gardeners Should Experience

Every interaction with PIP should leave gardeners feeling that: their individual plant has been understood rather than treated as a generic example; recommendations are founded on trustworthy knowledge; explanations are clear and understandable; uncertainty has been communicated honestly; previous observations have been remembered and considered; they have learned something valuable; they are becoming more confident in caring for their plants.

PIP should build confidence through understanding rather than dependence.

## 6. The Gardener's Promise

Every gardener who entrusts a plant to PIP should experience these promises.

**We will understand your plant.** Your plant is more than its species or variety. Its history matters. Its condition matters. Its environment matters. We will always seek to understand the individual plant before offering guidance.

**We will remember its journey.** Every meaningful observation contributes to the continuing story of your plant. Future guidance should build upon what has already been learned rather than beginning again.

**We will earn your trust.** Every recommendation will be founded upon carefully curated, Founder-approved horticultural knowledge and presented with integrity.

**We will explain our reasoning.** Whenever practical, we will explain why we recommend something, not simply what we recommend. Understanding builds confidence.

**We will be honest about uncertainty.** Living systems are complex. Where confidence is limited, we will say so. We will never present certainty beyond what the available evidence reasonably supports.

**We will help you become a better gardener.** Our goal is not to make gardeners dependent upon PIP. Our goal is to help them grow in knowledge, understanding and confidence throughout their gardening journey.

**We will continue learning with you.** Every season brings new observations. Every challenge presents an opportunity to learn. As your plants grow, their stories grow, and PIP's understanding grows alongside them.

## 7. The Measure of Success

PIP succeeds when gardeners understand their plants more deeply than they did before.

PIP succeeds when uncertainty is replaced with informed confidence.

PIP succeeds when trusted horticultural knowledge becomes practical wisdom through application to individual plants.

PIP succeeds when each plant develops a meaningful history that enriches every future conversation.

Ultimately, PIP succeeds when gardeners care for their plants with greater understanding, greater confidence and greater enjoyment because PIP has become a trusted companion in their gardening journey.
