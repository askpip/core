Chat GPT asked this question,

If in 20 years time, someone thanks us for PIP, What do I hope they are thanking us for?

I hope they will be thanking us for,

1. accepting the mantle placed upon us by the Lord to assemble and present the world with a high quality tool that will educate and assist people to not only care for their plants, but to understand them and steward them into the best possible health and production.

2. for keeping our integrity and humility. For always being open to advice and criticism, responding promptly and responsibly to credible advice, evidence and correction to ensure that PIP's information is accurate, professional, understandable and easily implemented and learned.

3. For their now being in a place where they seldom need to use PIP because they can simply look at their plants and Know confidently - what and where to prune and why. as well as other required information such as watering, soil care, companion plants, disease and pest remedy etc.

4. for the documented stored history of their journey with their plants growth and changes and the knowledge and understanding that has given them.

5. and for the cost of being a member of the PIP family being nothing compared to the value they have received.
