## Before God

We, the founders of this work, acknowledge that every good gift comes from God and that wisdom is something to be sought rather than assumed.

Before any software is written, before any business is established, and before any success is achieved, we desire to set our hearts in the right direction.

This covenant is not written to earn God's favour, but as an expression of our desire to honour Him in the work entrusted to us.

We recognise that this company ultimately belongs to Him more than it belongs to us.

We therefore choose to begin with humility, gratitude, and a commitment to faithful stewardship.

---

# Our Purpose

We desire to build tools that genuinely help people.

We desire to encourage thoughtful care for the living world and to make practical knowledge accessible to anyone willing to learn.

We recognise that financial success is a blessing when it is accompanied by integrity, generosity, and faithful stewardship.

We therefore commit ourselves to pursuing excellence without allowing profit to become our master.

---

# Our Commitment to Truth

We commit ourselves to seek truth in every area of this work.

We will not knowingly deceive our users.

We will not exaggerate the abilities of our products.

We will not make promises we cannot honestly support.

When we are uncertain, we will say so.

When we make mistakes, we will acknowledge them and correct them.

We desire to build a company whose reputation rests upon honesty rather than marketing.

---

# Our Commitment to Stewardship

We believe the earth and everything in it belongs to God.

We therefore recognise that plants are not merely products or resources.

They are part of His creation.

May every product we build encourage people to become wiser and more responsible caretakers.

May our work increase appreciation for creation rather than diminish it.

---

# Our Commitment to People

Every person who uses our products bears the image of God and deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

We therefore commit ourselves to:

* protect the privacy entrusted to us.
* collect only the information genuinely needed.
* communicate honestly.
* avoid manipulating people through fear, deception or unnecessary dependence.
* build products that educate rather than exploit.
* treat customers, employees, partners and suppliers fairly.
* listen with humility when others offer correction or wisdom.

---

# Our Commitment to Wisdom

We acknowledge that knowledge alone is not wisdom.

Artificial intelligence can process information.

It cannot replace discernment, integrity, compassion or conscience.

We therefore commit ourselves to seek wisdom through prayer, careful thought, wise counsel and continual learning before making significant decisions.

---

# Our Commitment to Excellence

Whatever work we undertake, we desire to do it wholeheartedly.

We will strive for quality in our products.

We will avoid unnecessary shortcuts that compromise trust.

We will continually improve what we build, recognising that excellence is a journey rather than a destination.

---

# Our Commitment to One Another

As founders, we commit ourselves to treat one another with honesty, patience and grace.

We will seek unity rather than personal recognition.

We will speak truthfully, even when conversations are difficult.

We will seek reconciliation quickly when disagreements arise.

We recognise that preserving our relationship is more valuable than winning an argument.

---

# Our Commitment to Steward Resources Wisely

If God chooses to bless this work financially, we commit ourselves to remember that prosperity brings responsibility.

We will seek to use the resources entrusted to us wisely.

We desire to create value before seeking reward.

We desire to bless others rather than merely enrich ourselves.

We recognise that every success carries with it a greater responsibility to serve faithfully.

---

# Our Prayer

Father,

Grant us wisdom beyond our own understanding.

Keep us humble when things go well.

Keep us faithful when they do not.

Protect us from pride, greed and compromise.

Help us build something that genuinely serves people and reflects integrity in every decision.

Give us discernment to know when to move forward, when to wait, and when to change direction.

May this work encourage people to care more thoughtfully for the world You have made.

May we never value success more highly than faithfulness.

And whatever becomes of this project, may our character honour You more than our achievements ever could.

In the name of Jesus Christ,

Amen.

---

# Our Covenant

As founders, we freely make these commitments before God and before one another.

We understand that we will not keep them perfectly.

When we fail, we will seek forgiveness, make things right where we are able, and continue forward with humility.

May every major decision we make be measured against this covenant.

May our work always remain an act of faithful stewardship.
