## A Note from the Founders

Every worthwhile endeavour begins with a reason.

This company did not begin with software, artificial intelligence, or the desire to build another application.

It began with a conviction.

We believe the living world is worthy of understanding, respect, and thoughtful care.

We believe knowledge should be shared generously.

We believe technology should help people become wiser rather than more dependent.

This Charter exists so that, regardless of how large this company becomes or how technology changes, the principles upon which it was founded remain constant.

Everything we build should honour these principles.

If ever we are forced to choose between rapid growth and remaining faithful to them, we choose the principles.

They are the foundation upon which this company stands.

## The Founders' Conviction

As founders, we acknowledge that our understanding of the world is shaped by our belief that creation is neither accidental nor without purpose.

We believe humanity has been entrusted with the responsibility of caring wisely for the living world rather than exploiting it carelessly.

This conviction shapes how we think, how we design, and how we conduct ourselves.

At the same time, we recognise that people from many different cultures, backgrounds and beliefs share a genuine love for plants and a desire to care for them well.

Our purpose is not to persuade people to adopt our beliefs.

Our purpose is to serve them with honesty, excellence, humility and respect.

We hope that the values reflected in our work speak more loudly than any statement we could make.

## Our Purpose

We exist to help people understand and care for the living world through trustworthy knowledge, thoughtful technology, and practical guidance.

Every recommendation should increase confidence.

Every interaction should increase understanding.

Every improvement should strengthen people's ability to care wisely for the plants entrusted to them.

## Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted source of practical plant intelligence, helping people care for gardens, orchards, landscapes and ecosystems with confidence, understanding and respect.

## Our Mission

To combine careful observation, proven horticultural knowledge and intelligent technology into tools that help people understand what their plants need, why they need it, and how best to care for them.

## Our Three Pillars

### 1. Stewardship

The living world deserves thoughtful care.

Our work should encourage responsible stewardship rather than careless use.

Every recommendation should seek the long-term wellbeing of the plant wherever practical.

### 2. Truth

Truth is the foundation of trust.

We will never knowingly exaggerate the certainty of our recommendations.

When evidence is strong, we will speak with confidence.

When evidence is incomplete, we will acknowledge uncertainty.

We would rather admit "we do not know" than pretend to know.

### 3. Service

Technology exists to serve people.

Every interaction should leave users feeling more confident, more capable and more respected than before.

## Our Core Values

**Integrity** — We will act honestly even when honesty is inconvenient.

**Humility** — Knowledge grows. We remain teachable. We welcome correction. We recognise that no individual, expert or artificial intelligence possesses complete knowledge.

**Excellence** — We pursue quality because people place their trust in our guidance. That trust deserves our very best.

**Simplicity** — Complex knowledge should be communicated simply. The platform should reduce confusion, not create it.

**Respect** — Every person deserves patience. Every plant deserves thoughtful consideration. Every interaction should reflect kindness.

**Responsibility** — Our recommendations have real-world consequences. We will design our systems carefully, test them thoroughly and improve them continually.

## Our Design Principles

**Teach Before Telling** — Every recommendation should educate. Understanding creates confidence. Confidence creates capable caretakers.

**Explain Every Decision** — No recommendation should ever be a mystery. Users deserve to understand why guidance has been given.

**Protect Living Things** — If uncertainty exists, choose the safest course. When confidence is insufficient, request more information rather than risk unnecessary harm.

**Separate Knowledge from Technology** — Software changes. Knowledge grows. The two should evolve independently.

**Design for Decades** — Every decision should strengthen the platform for future generations. Short-term convenience must never undermine long-term quality.

**Earn Trust Daily** — Trust is never permanent. It is earned through thousands of honest interactions.

## Our Commitment to Users

Every person who entrusts us with information deserves respect. We commit ourselves to:

protecting personal information with care; collecting only the information genuinely required to provide the requested service; being transparent about how information is used; giving users meaningful control over their own information wherever practical; never selling personal conversations, photographs or garden histories; continually improving security as technology evolves; communicating clearly when limitations or risks exist.

Trust is not merely a technical responsibility. It is a moral one.

## Our Commitment to Knowledge

Knowledge should become more accurate over time. Every improvement should be based upon evidence, careful observation and responsible review.

Artificial intelligence may assist us. It must never replace thoughtful judgement.

The platform should always remain explainable. People deserve to understand the reasoning behind recommendations that may affect living things.

## Our Commitment to the Community

We believe knowledge grows stronger when shared responsibly. We will seek the wisdom of gardeners, horticulturalists, botanists, orchardists, researchers and experienced practitioners. We welcome respectful feedback. We acknowledge mistakes. We correct them openly.

Where appropriate, we hope to build opportunities for users to contribute observations, photographs, regional knowledge and practical experience in ways that strengthen the platform for everyone, while always respecting their ownership, privacy and consent.

This platform should grow through partnership rather than ownership.

## Our Commitment to Future Generations

Technology changes rapidly. Principles should not.

This Charter is intended to guide decisions long after its founders are gone.

Future leaders should be free to improve the platform, adopt new technologies and expand its capabilities. They should never compromise the principles upon which it was founded.

## The Legacy We Hope to Leave

If this company succeeds, we hope it is remembered not because it built sophisticated technology, but because it helped people understand and care for the living world more wisely.

If future generations inherit this work, may they value:

Truth above convenience. Stewardship above exploitation. Understanding above assumption. Service above recognition. Integrity above profit.

May every improvement strengthen both the platform and the people who use it. May knowledge be shared generously. May trust always be earned. And may the work remain worthy of the confidence placed in it.

We recognise that our understanding is limited. We therefore commit ourselves to continual learning, careful observation, humility, and responsible stewardship. Our hope is that this platform helps people better understand and care for the living world, while remembering that all wisdom ultimately has its source beyond ourselves.

## Appendix — Companion Texts

These short texts were drafted alongside the Charter, in the same Workspace, and express the same founding conviction in condensed form. They are preserved here for reference rather than as separate controlled documents.

### The PIP 5 Pillars

1. Truth over appearance.
2. Stewardship over exploitation.
3. Education over dependence.
4. Service before profit.
5. Long-term trust over short-term growth.

### The PIP Promise

We promise to treat every plant as worthy of thoughtful care.

We promise to treat every user with honesty, respect and humility.

We promise to explain rather than merely instruct.

We promise to admit uncertainty rather than pretend certainty.

We promise to continue learning so that those who learn from us may one day no longer need us.

We promise that trust will always be more valuable than growth, and integrity more valuable than profit.

> A seed grows into a plant.
> Knowledge grows into wisdom.
> Confidence grows into stewardship.
> A gardener grows over a lifetime.

### The Plant Intelligence Manifesto

We are not building software that knows about plants. We are building software that learns to understand plants so it can help people understand them too.

We do not believe people need more information. They need better understanding.

We are not building another plant identification app. We are building a platform that helps people understand the living things they care for.

Artificial intelligence is not our product. It is one of the tools we use. Knowledge is our product. Trust is our foundation. Education is our purpose. Stewardship is our responsibility.

Every recommendation should leave the user more capable than before. Every improvement should strengthen understanding rather than dependence.

We measure success not only by how accurately the platform identifies plants, but by how many people become wiser caretakers because they used it.

Technology will continue to change. Our commitment to truth, stewardship and service should not.
