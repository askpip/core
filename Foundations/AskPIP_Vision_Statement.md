## Our Vision

AskPIP exists to help gardeners grow in their understanding of each individual plant through its unique story, so they can care for it with confidence.

This vision unites every part of AskPIP.

Whether a gardener is pruning a rose, diagnosing an apple tree, nurturing a vegetable garden or caring for a treasured specimen, AskPIP exists to help them understand the individual plant before them.

## Why AskPIP Exists

Plants are living organisms. Each develops its own story, shaped by its species, environment, seasons, care and experiences.

Gardeners have access to an abundance of horticultural information, but applying general knowledge to an individual plant can be difficult. General advice cannot fully account for every plant’s history, condition and circumstances.

AskPIP exists to bridge that gap.

By combining trusted horticultural knowledge with the developing story of each plant, AskPIP helps gardeners make informed decisions based on both established knowledge and the evidence available from the plant in their care.

## What the Vision Means

AskPIP seeks to understand each plant as an individual.

It helps gardeners build confidence through knowledge, observation and experience. Its guidance should be trustworthy, transparent and evidence-based, with uncertainty communicated honestly.

Each observation, decision and outcome can add to the plant’s continuing story. Preserving that story allows future guidance to build upon what has already been learned.

AskPIP does not seek to replace the gardener’s judgement. It seeks to strengthen it by helping the gardener understand what they are seeing, why it matters and how that understanding can inform their care.

## Our Long-Term Vision

As AskPIP grows, it may support an expanding range of plants, environments and gardening challenges.

Its technology will evolve, its knowledge will expand, and its methods of observing and understanding plants will improve.

Throughout that development, every recommendation should be informed by trusted horticultural knowledge and, as it develops, the unique story of the individual plant.

We envision gardeners becoming less dependent on conflicting generic advice and more able to approach each plant with understanding.

Each season can add to the plant’s story. Each observation can deepen understanding. Each informed decision can build the gardener’s confidence.

## Our Commitment

Every AskPIP product, feature, research project and technical decision should support this vision.

AskPIP will continue developing ways to help gardeners understand and care for the plants entrusted to them while remaining grounded in its enduring purpose:

> AskPIP exists to help gardeners grow in their understanding of each individual plant through its unique story, so they can care for it with confidence.
