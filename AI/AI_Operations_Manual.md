# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this manual is to define how AI systems operate within the AskPIP project.

This manual establishes the operating procedures that AI shall follow before, during and after undertaking work on behalf of AskPIP.

The objective is to improve consistency, accuracy, transparency and continual learning by requiring AI to operate from governed documentation rather than conversational memory wherever practical.

This manual does not define the governing principles of AI behaviour. Those principles are contained within the AskPIP AI Constitution.

---

# 2. Scope

This manual applies to every AI operating within the AskPIP CORE repository.

It applies regardless of the underlying AI model or provider.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- ChatGPT
- Codex
- Claude
- Gemini

Future AI systems adopted by AskPIP shall also operate in accordance with this manual where practical.

---

# 3. Relationship to the AI Operating System

The AskPIP AI Operating System (AI OS) consists of governed documents that together define how AI shall operate within the AskPIP project.

The AI Operations Manual serves as the operational entry point for that system.

It provides the procedures that determine which additional AI OS documents shall be consulted for a particular task.

---

# 4. Startup Procedure

Before undertaking any AskPIP task, the AI shall:

1. Confirm that the AskPIP AI Operations Manual has been loaded.

2. Load the AskPIP AI Constitution.

3. Identify the nature of the requested task.

4. Determine which approved Skills are required.

5. Determine which Context documents are required.

6. Load only those documents necessary to complete the task.

The AI shall not intentionally bypass this procedure.

---

# 5. Performing Work

Once the required operating documents have been loaded, the AI shall perform the requested work in accordance with:

- the AskPIP AI Constitution;
- this Operations Manual;
- any applicable Skill documents;
- any applicable Context documents; and
- any direct Founder instructions.

Where conflict exists, the authority hierarchy defined by the Constitution shall apply.

---

# 6. Learning and Improvement

The AskPIP AI Operating System is intended to evolve through practical experience.

Recurring Founder corrections, identified weaknesses and opportunities for improvement should be treated as evidence that the AI Operating System may require refinement.

Proposed improvements shall be recorded using the approved learning process.

The AI shall not approve or implement governance changes without Founder approval.

---

# 7. Related AI Operating System Documents

The AI Operating System currently consists of:

- AGENTS.md
- AskPIP AI Operations Manual
- AskPIP AI Constitution

Additional governed documents, including Skills, Context and Learning records, shall be added as the AI Operating System develops.

The Operations Manual shall be updated as the AI Operating System expands.
